Wai Koa Loop Trail

Anaina Hou Community Park is the northernmost trailhead for the Wai Koa Loop, on the page the park publishes for the trail. Hours are 9:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. The page says the route is welcomed by North Shore landscapes, open pastures, and a shaded mahogany forest, and that it leads to the historic Kīlauea Stone Dam, an 1880s landmark with cascading water. It gives the distance as approximately 4.3 miles round trip, about 1.5 to 2 hours, beginner and intermediate, and mostly flat. It says the trail is protected by the Hawaii Land Trust.

A waiver must be completed before entering. The park page links a form and also says a waiver is available at the trailhead. Walkers are told to bring shoes for dirt and mud, water, and bug spray, because the land is agricultural. The park’s published address is 5-2723 Kuhio Highway, Kilauea, HI 96754, and its phone number is (808) 828-2118. This guide does not treat a visitor review on that page as the park’s description of swimming.

Hawaii Land Trust’s own trail page lists the Wai Koa Loop Trail at Kalihiwai, Haleleʻa. The year protected is 2019, through a conservation easement. Listed features are a walking trail, a Kilauea stone dam built in the 1880s, mahogany trees, and a pond system. The trust’s Kauai lands page sizes the same place as a 5 mile hiking trail and adds an organic orchard, a food forest, and catch-and-release fish ponds. The 4.3-mile figure and the 5-mile figure are both printed by the organizations that manage or host the walk. This guide does not average them, and it does not call the forest the largest mahogany stand in the United States.

The pages do not say the waiver has been dropped, and they do not name a dog park as the way in.

The dam has its own page: Kīlauea Stone Dam.

Last updated 21 October 2026.

Questions

How long is the loop?

Anaina Hou says approximately 4.3 miles round trip, about 1.5 to 2 hours. Hawaii Land Trust’s Kauai lands page says a 5 mile hiking trail. Both figures are kept.

Do I need a waiver?

Anaina Hou says a waiver must be completed before entering, through the form linked on its trail page or at the trailhead. Trailhead hours on that page are 9:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.
