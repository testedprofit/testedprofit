Charles Lindbergh's grave

The church yard

Wikipedia's article on Charles Lindbergh says he was buried on the grounds of Palapala Hoʻomau Church in Kipahulu, Maui. The caption on the grave photograph in that article names the same church. The article says he died on the morning of 26 August 1974, at age 72, and it quotes a biography that says he was buried on Maui that day in a grave he helped design in the traditional Hawaiian style. It says he flew to Honolulu on 17 August and then to Maui, and died a week later. It also says he and his friend Samuel F. Pryor Jr. agreed to make their final resting place in the small cemetery they cleared while restoring the church.

What the church says about the building

The church's own history page says missionaries from New England reached Kipahulu in 1864 and built Palapala Hoʻomau of limestone coral and lava rock, in the form of the meeting houses they knew at home. It says the graveyard became the resting place for local Hawaiian families and for Chinese workers from the nearby sugar plantation. It says the church was later abandoned, and that in 1964 Sam Pryor found it in the jungle and began restoring the building and the graveyard. He added a stained-glass window of a Polynesian Christ draped in the red-and-yellow feather capes reserved for Hawaii's highest chiefs. The first service since the 1940s, the page says, was on Thanksgiving Day 1965. The contact page says a small endowment set up by the Lindbergh and Pryor families, administered by the Hawaiʻi Conference Foundation, paid for upkeep for many years, and that the Palapala Hoʻomau Preservation Society was created in 2012. The history page does not itself describe Lindbergh's grave. The burial place in this guide comes from the Wikipedia article, which cites the church pages for the building and the endowment.

Finding the church

The church visit page says Palapala Hoʻomau is eight miles south of Hana, just past the 41-mile marker. Travelers turn left at the signs for the church and for Maui Stables. From Kaupo, the signs are just past the Laulima Farms stand: an unpaved road on the ocean side, then left at the fork, to a small parking area. The page asks visitors to walk lightly, not to disturb furniture, and not to step on graves, including ones that are barely marked. It also warns against the cliff edge. The church pages say 1864 and give the mile-marker directions instead.

Questions

Where is Charles Lindbergh buried?

Wikipedia says he was buried on the grounds of Palapala Hoʻomau Church in Kipahulu, Maui, on 26 August 1974. The church visit page places the church eight miles south of Hana, just past the 41-mile marker.

When was the church built?

The church history page says missionaries built Palapala Hoʻomau in 1864 of limestone coral and lava rock. It says Sam Pryor began a restoration in 1964, and that the first service since the 1940s was on Thanksgiving Day 1965.
