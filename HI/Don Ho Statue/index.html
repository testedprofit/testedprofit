Don Ho

The sculptor's record

Kim Duffett's page for the piece titles it I Remember You and lists the bronze as 76 inches by 26 inches by 20 inches. It dates Don Ho from 13 August 1930 to 14 April 2007. It says he became famous with his band the Aliis at Duke's nightclub in the original International Marketplace, and it names Tiny Bubbles and I'll Remember You among the songs. It says Taubman Centers Inc. commissioned the sculpture for the International Market Place, that it was completed in August 2017, and that the work received an Urban Land Institute Global Award for Excellence in 2019. Those are the measurements, the dates, and the client on the sculptor's page. The page does not give a street number, a weight, or a ceremony time.

The market place, and the lines that did not load

Wikipedia's article on the International Market Place calls it an open-air shopping center in Waikīkī on Oʻahu. It says the center first opened in 1956, closed for a complete renovation in 2013, and reopened on 25 August 2016. It says revenues support The Queen's Medical Center. That article does not mention the Don Ho sculpture. The sculptor's page and the encyclopedia article together place a 2017 bronze at a Waikiki center that had reopened the year before. They do not, by themselves, describe a banyan tree, an 87th-birthday unveiling, or a pound weight. A Business Wire release, the Go Hawaii listing, and a Honolulu Star-Advertiser story about the statue did not return a readable copy on this check and are not linked. The older page's 300-pound weight, the address 2324 Kalākaua Avenue, a round-the-clock schedule, and the birthday-unveiling line are not in the two pages that loaded, so they are left out. Seventy-six inches is 6 feet 4 inches. That conversion is arithmetic from the sculptor's height, not a second measurement from a newspaper.

Questions

Who made the Don Ho bronze, and how tall is it?

Kim Duffett lists a bronze 76 inches by 26 inches by 20 inches, commissioned by Taubman Centers Inc. for the International Market Place and completed in August 2017. The page dates Don Ho from 13 August 1930 to 14 April 2007.

Where is the International Market Place?

Wikipedia calls it an open-air shopping center in Waikīkī that reopened on 25 August 2016. The sculptor's page places the bronze there. Neither page prints a street number.
