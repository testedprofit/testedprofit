Fagan's Cross

The cross on Lyon Hill

Wikipedia's article on Fagan's Cross calls it a large stone cross on Lyon Hill in Hana, Hawaii, created in 1960 and made from volcanic rock. It says the cross is 545 feet, or 166 meters, above sea level. That figure is an elevation, not the height of the cross itself. The article says the cross was made in 1960 in memory of Paul Irving Fagan, Sr., a San Francisco native who died in 1960 and who had established cattle farming in Hana. It says he was not buried near the cross. His grave is at Cypress Lawn Memorial Park in California. It also says residents of Hana gather at the cross before sunrise on Easter Sunday for mass.

The ranch, under more than one hotel name

The Hana Ranch about page says that in 1944 Mr. Fagan acquired 14,000 acres in Hāna, brought cattle from Molokaʻi, and started Hāna Ranch. It says the Kaʻuiki Inn, opened by Paul Fagan in 1946, was later called the Hotel Hāna-Maui, and that the last sugar plantation closed in the same year the inn opened. Wikipedia says Fagan built the town's main hotel in 1946 and calls that hotel Travaasa Hana, now owned by Hyatt. This guide keeps the ranch page's Kaʻuiki Inn and Hotel Hāna-Maui beside Wikipedia's Travaasa Hana. It does not treat the 14,000 acres of 1944 as the size of the ranch today. The same ranch page says Hāna Ranch Stewards bought the main mauka portion in January 2014, roughly 3,600 acres above the Hāna Highway, plus the cattle operation and the water utilities. The ranch page does not mention the cross.

The walk Wikipedia describes

Wikipedia says the cross can be reached by a 1.5-mile round trip with 500 feet of elevation gain, and that the trailhead is across from the Travaasa Hana hotel. It also says the trail leads to a viewpoint of Maui's eastern coast. A Hawaii Magazine sunrise story did not return a readable copy on this check and is not linked, so this guide does not add that story's private-land warning or a street address for the hotel. The older page's claim that the cross itself is roughly 18 feet tall is not the 545-foot elevation in the article, and no loaded page gives a height for the cross, so the 18-foot line is left out.

Questions

What is Fagan's Cross, and how high is the hill?

Wikipedia calls it a volcanic-rock cross on Lyon Hill in Hana, created in 1960 in memory of Paul Irving Fagan, Sr. It says the cross stands 545 feet above sea level. That is the elevation, not a measured height of the cross.

How long is the walk?

Wikipedia says the trail is 1.5 miles round trip with 500 feet of elevation gain, starting across from the Travaasa Hana hotel. The Hana Ranch page uses the earlier names Kaʻuiki Inn and Hotel Hāna-Maui for the 1946 hotel.
