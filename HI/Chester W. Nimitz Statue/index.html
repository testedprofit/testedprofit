Chester W. Nimitz statue

The dedication DVIDS reported

A Defense Visual Information Distribution Service story says Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam marked the 68th anniversary of the end of World War II on Monday, 2 September, at the Battleship Missouri Memorial. The story's date taken is 2 September 2013. After the ceremony, it says, dignitaries dedicated a statue of Fleet Admiral Chester W. Nimitz. It calls the work a 9-foot bronze that will stand along the pier where USS Missouri (BB 63) is berthed. The photograph captions place the unveiling at the Battleship Missouri Memorial in Pearl Harbor. The story does not name the sculptor.

What the Naval Order page adds

The Naval Order of the United States project page, carrying the date 22 October 2015, describes a 9 foot bronze statue and a black granite pedestal to be permanently installed on the shore side of Pier Foxtrot Five on Ford Island, where USS Missouri is berthed. It says the pier is just aft of the USS Arizona Memorial, on the shoreline once known as battleship row, and that over 500,000 people visit the battleship each year. It says the figure shows the admiral as he appeared at the battle of the Marshall Islands in February 1944: a wash khaki short-sleeve shirt, no medals or campaign ribbons, rank insignia only, and a belt buckle with twin dolphins. A horse shoe leans against the base. The page spells those two words as horse shoe and past time, and it calls the horse shoe his favorite pastime. The pedestal, it says, is absolute black granite, the same granite used for the Vietnam Veterans Memorial wall's material in that account, and the quotation is in gold leaf. The page prints the lines without a closing quotation mark: They fought together as brothers in arms. They died together and now they sleep side by side. To them we have a solemn obligation. The obligation to ensure that their sacrifice will help to make this a better and safer world in which to live.

DVIDS reports a dedication on 2 September 2013. The Naval Order page, dated 22 October 2015, still describes the installation in the future tense. This guide does not pick one tense and hide the other. Both pages agree on a 9-foot bronze at the Missouri's pier. The Naval Order page adds Pier Foxtrot Five, the black granite, the 1944 khakis, the horse shoe, and the quotation. Neither page prints a street address, gate hours, or a sculptor's name. to 4 p.m. schedule, a shuttle-only rule, a count of 2.5 million sailors, and a 7 December 1941 appointment are not in the two pages. The battleship's own homepage, as it loaded, does not mention the statue or print visiting hours.

Questions

When was the Nimitz statue dedicated, and how tall is it?

DVIDS dates the dedication at the Battleship Missouri Memorial to 2 September 2013 and calls the statue a 9-foot bronze. The Naval Order project page also says 9 foot and describes a black granite pedestal at Pier Foxtrot Five, in wording that still treats the installation as forthcoming.

What is the figure wearing?

The Naval Order page says the statue shows Nimitz at the battle of the Marshall Islands in February 1944, in a wash khaki short-sleeve shirt with rank insignia and a submarine-qualification belt buckle. A horse shoe leans against the base.
