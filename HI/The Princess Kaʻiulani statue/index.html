Princess Kaʻiulani

The Historic Hawaii Foundation walking-tour sheet for statues in Waikīkī lists Princess Victoria Kaʻiulani, 1875 to 1899. The artist on that sheet is Jan Gordon Fisher. The sheet says the statue was erected in 1999 at Kaʻiulani Triangle, at Kūhiō and Kaʻiulani Avenues. It lists the address as 150 Kaʻiulani Avenue. It says Outrigger Enterprises and the Kelley Family commissioned it, as a gift to the City and County of Honolulu. The sheet’s research note credits Cheryl Soon and a 2022 Mutual Publishing book.

Outrigger’s newsroom, in a piece dated 20 October 2019, says that in 1999 Outrigger commissioned the Princess Kaʻiulani statue and has held a lei-draping ceremony every year since, the 20th annual as of that article. The ceremony honors the princess, whose years the piece gives as 1875 to 1899. It says the ceremony was held on Wednesday morning, 16 October, at 10 a.m., at the statue on the corner of Kaʻiulani and Kuhio Avenues, across from the OHANA Waikiki East by Outrigger. The program was free and open to the public. The 16 October date in that piece is the 2019 ceremony. The piece says the ceremony has been held every year since 1999. It does not say that 16 October was the original dedication day.

The walking-tour sheet is the source for the sculptor’s name, Jan Gordon Fisher, and for the 1999 erection date. The pages do not describe a book, a lei of pikake, or a life-size measurement. The statue is in Waikiki, at the triangle named in the sheet and at the corner named by Outrigger.

The same walking-tour sheet is also the source used for the Queen Kapiʻolani statue in Kapiʻolani Park. The two entries are separate.

Last updated 21 October 2026.

Questions

Who made the statue, and when?

The walking-tour sheet names Jan Gordon Fisher and says the statue was erected in 1999 at Kaʻiulani Triangle. Outrigger’s 2019 newsroom piece also says Outrigger commissioned the statue in 1999.

Where is the ceremony held?

Outrigger’s 20 October 2019 piece places the lei draping at the statue on the corner of Kaʻiulani and Kuhio Avenues, across from the OHANA Waikiki East by Outrigger. The 16 October date in that piece is the 2019 ceremony.
