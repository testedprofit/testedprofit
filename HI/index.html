Hawaii landmark guides

This page is the index of Hawaii landmark guides on TestedProfit. Twenty of them are filed under the Hawaii directory. Ten of those were rewritten on 21 October 2026 from public records, and their cards use the new titles. The second group still uses the current titles of the ten Hawaii guides that have not been revised yet. A third card opens the Captain Cook statue page for Waimea, Kauai. That page lives outside this folder, at its own URL, and this pass did not rewrite it. The old directory blurb for a Waimea Cook statue was pointing at a real page. The card now uses that page's title.

Cards are titles only. A blurb under a card would repeat claims that the later pages have not been checked. When a later pass revises one of those guides, the card title should be replaced with the new title. Earning-app guides and the national park archive are linked from the related list. They are not mixed into these cards.

Questions

What does this directory include?

It lists every guide filed in the Hawaii directory, twenty pages, plus the Captain Cook statue page for Waimea, which is filed outside this folder. Ten guides were revised on 21 October 2026. The other cards still use the titles those pages have now.

Do the cards match the guides?

Each card uses the title of the page it opens. Revised guides use their new titles. Pages left for a later pass keep the titles they have now, including the Waimea page.
