Secretariat in a Lexington roundabout

WKYT's story dated 14 October 2019 says a statue of Triple Crown winner Secretariat is now at a Lexington traffic circle. The station says the 3,800-pound statue was installed Monday at Alexandria Drive and Old Frankfort Pike. The story's publication date is 14 October 2019.

Size and the sculptor

WKYT says the statue is 21 feet from hoof to hoof and more than 11 feet tall. Washington sculptor Jocelyn Russell made the statue in Oklahoma, and it was transported to Lexington the week before the story. It took nearly 16 months to complete. Several people watched the installation.

What the intersection is

The place WKYT names is a traffic circle in Lexington, Kentucky, where Alexandria Drive meets Old Frankfort Pike. The station says several people watched the installation and many took pictures of what it called Lexington's newest landmark. The story's time stamp is 3:49 p.m. EDT on 14 October 2019.
