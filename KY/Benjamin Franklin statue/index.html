Benjamin Franklin statue

Wikipedia's article on the sculptor Felix de Weldon lists a 1973 Benjamin Franklin at the Louisville Public Library in Louisville, Kentucky. The same article lists a separate 1973 work, General George Rogers Clark, also in Louisville. This guide is about the library sculpture. The Clark sculpture is the bronze at Riverfront Plaza/Belvedere, with its own downtown listing that names Felix W. de Weldon, the Hillman-Hopkins gift, and the address 485 West Main Street.

The Franklin entry names the Louisville Public Library and the year 1973. Both works share that year on the de Weldon list, and they honor different people. The library is the place the article gives for the Franklin. The Belvedere listing is the place to read the Clark sculpture's site, sponsor, and street address.

Felix de Weldon's article is the account of the Franklin sculpture on this page. Visitors looking for the riverfront figure can use the Clark guide linked below. The article lists two 1973 works in Louisville: Benjamin Franklin at the public library, and General George Rogers Clark.

The Clark record is on the George Rogers Clark guide.
