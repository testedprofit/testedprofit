Golden David at 21c Louisville

Serkan Özkaya's David (inspired by Michelangelo) stands outside 21c Museum Hotel in Louisville. 21c's museum page calls it a double-size golden replica of Michelangelo's David, made for the 9th International Istanbul Biennial in 2005. Days before the biennial opened, the sculpture collapsed shortly after installation. Özkaya restored the damaged replica and cast two additional copies. 21c acquired one in 2010.

The trip to Louisville

21c says that in 2011 it commissioned the 30-foot sculpture's journey from Istanbul to Louisville, with a stop in New York. Wikipedia says the figure is gold-painted foam, twice the size of the original, based on a computer model by Stanford professor Marc Levoy, and that six people worked for six months before it collapsed during installation. Wikipedia says two copies were cast in Eskişehir, one of them acquired by 21c, and that the Louisville copy was carried lying on its side into New York in March 2011 for a presentation at the Storefront for Art and Architecture.

Main and Seventh

Louisville Public Media, in a story published 2 May 2012, says the oversized David was installed that day on the corner of Main and Seventh streets. It arrived on a truck and was stood on an 8-foot pedestal with a crane. The station calls it three stories tall, with gold paint, and says it would stay on long-term display outside the downtown museum. Curator Alice Gray Stites is quoted on the work as a way for the public to meet the ideas behind the collection. Özkaya says everything about it is a copy, not an original, and that it is not gold, only painted. Wikipedia's account calls the material gold-painted foam.

Questions

When did David arrive at 21c?

Louisville Public Media says Serkan Özkaya's David was installed on 2 May 2012 at the corner of Main and Seventh streets, lifted onto an 8-foot pedestal by a crane. 21c says it acquired a copy in 2010 and commissioned the sculpture's journey from Istanbul to Louisville in 2011.

What is it made of?

21c calls it a double-size golden replica of Michelangelo's David. Özkaya told Louisville Public Media it is a copy, not an original, and that it is not gold, only painted. Wikipedia calls it gold-painted foam, twice the size of the original, from a computer model by Stanford professor Marc Levoy.
