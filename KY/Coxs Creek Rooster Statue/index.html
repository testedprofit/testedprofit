Ozzie Frank at Rooster Run

Wikipedia says Rooster Run is a general store in Nelson County, Kentucky, opened by Joe Evans in 1967, when it was called Evans Beverage Depot. He opened it with 200 dollars of his own and 200 dollars from his father. At the time it was the only place in the county selling alcohol. The article says that one evening after closing, a wife arrived and a man ran, and a friend said, Look at that rooster run. Evans's mother then sewed caps with the name Rooster Run. Evans trademarked the name and the logo in 1986. A 13.5-foot, or 4.1-metre, fiberglass rooster dubbed Ozzie Frank was placed in front of the store.

Wikipedia says the caps stayed the most popular item, that Evans had a standing order for 30,000 caps a quarter, and that by 1991 he estimated he had sold about 1.5 million. The locale is on state highway maps. Mail addressed to Rooster Run is delivered if it uses the Cox's Creek ZIP code. Evans convinced the Transportation Cabinet to put signs along Kentucky Route 245. In the mid-1980s he was elected a Nelson County magistrate. In 1991 he legally changed his name to Joe Rooster Run Evans, ran for Secretary of State, toured with Ozzie Frank, and finished last in a three-man Democratic primary. In 1998 he lost the Kentucky Senate 8th District race to Dan Kelly. Wikipedia says he retired in 2004 and died in 2013.

The Kentucky Standard, in a story by Katelyn Norris dated 27 July 2024, says Ozzie Frank was missing that spring and came back with a fresh coat of paint for the reopening. New owners Tim Hutchins, Muni Patel, and Kash Patel brought the store back after a closure the story places in the late 2010s. The story says the store honors the late Joe Evans, that the dining-room wall holds clippings, and that the stop is on Ky. 245. General manager Brooke Hutchins said the staff prepared a 4 a.m. opening on June 26, the day the story says the new store opened, which it also calls late the previous month. The story announces a grand-opening weekend of 23 to 25 August. Both pages spell the rooster's name Ozzie Frank.

Questions

What is Ozzie Frank?

Wikipedia says a 13.5-foot, or 4.1-metre, fiberglass rooster dubbed Ozzie Frank stands in front of the Rooster Run store. A 27 July 2024 Kentucky Standard story says the rooster was missing in the spring and came back with fresh paint for the reopening.

When did the store open, and when did it reopen?

Wikipedia says Joe Evans opened Evans Beverage Depot in 1967. The Kentucky Standard story of 27 July 2024 says the new owners opened the doors on June 26, which that story places in the previous month, and that the staff prepared a 4 a.m. opening that day.
