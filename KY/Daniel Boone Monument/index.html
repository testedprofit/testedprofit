Daniel Boone statue

Olmsted Parks Conservancy lists a Daniel Boone statue among the points of interest in Cherokee Park. The listing says the sculpture by Enid Yandell was commissioned by the Filson Club to stand in front of the Kentucky building at the 1893 Chicago World's Fair. It says C.C. Bickel arranged to have the work cast in bronze for the city of Louisville in 1906. Those are the two dates on the page that opened: the 1893 commission, and the 1906 bronze casting. This guide does not add other years.

The same park listing prints Cherokee Park, 745 Cochran Hill Road, Louisville, Kentucky 40206. That line is the park address attached to the points of interest, including this statue. It is not, on this page, a separate street number for the sculpture alone. A limited-mobility parking note from another Olmsted page was not in the file that opened, so it is not repeated.

A Library of Congress photograph note returned 403. An SAH Archipedia entry for Cherokee Park returned 403. A University of Louisville digital-library image failed to download. Those three are named and not linked, and this guide does not copy a height, a pedestal description, or a street number from them. The Olmsted text does not call the work a stone cairn, and it does not give a height. Visitors who want the Eastern Parkway entrance named as the statue's site will not find that sentence in the page that loaded. The bronze casting for the city is the 1906 line. The 1893 line is the fair commission.

Questions

Who made the Cherokee Park Boone, and when?

Olmsted Parks says Enid Yandell made the sculpture, that the Filson Club commissioned it for the Kentucky building at the 1893 Chicago World's Fair, and that C.C. Bickel arranged a bronze casting for Louisville in 1906.

What is the street address?

The same Olmsted listing gives Cherokee Park as 745 Cochran Hill Road, Louisville, Kentucky 40206. That is the park address on the listing, not a separate statue street number. A Library of Congress item and an SAH Archipedia entry did not open, and a university image failed to download, so height and extra dates from those pages are not used.
