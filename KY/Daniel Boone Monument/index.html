Daniel Boone statue

Olmsted Parks Conservancy lists a Daniel Boone statue among the points of interest in Cherokee Park. The listing says the sculpture by Enid Yandell was commissioned by the Filson Club to stand in front of the Kentucky building at the 1893 Chicago World's Fair. It says C.C. Bickel arranged to have the work cast in bronze for the city of Louisville in 1906. The conservancy gives those two dates: the 1893 fair commission, and the 1906 bronze casting for the city.

The same park listing prints Cherokee Park, 745 Cochran Hill Road, Louisville, Kentucky 40206. That is the address Olmsted Parks prints with the Cherokee Park points of interest, including the Daniel Boone statue. Enid Yandell is the sculptor named on the listing. The Filson Club is the group that commissioned the fair sculpture, and C.C. Bickel is the person who arranged the later bronze for Louisville.

Visitors can read the statue among the other Cherokee Park points of interest on the Olmsted Parks page. The fair appearance and the city bronze are both part of that account. The park address on the listing is 745 Cochran Hill Road in Louisville.

Questions

Who made the Cherokee Park Boone, and when?

Olmsted Parks says Enid Yandell made the sculpture, that the Filson Club commissioned it for the Kentucky building at the 1893 Chicago World's Fair, and that C.C. Bickel arranged a bronze casting for Louisville in 1906.

What is the street address?

Olmsted Parks prints Cherokee Park, 745 Cochran Hill Road, Louisville, Kentucky 40206, with the points of interest that include the Daniel Boone statue.
