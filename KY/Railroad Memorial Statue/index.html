Train conductor in Carbondale

The conductor statue on this page stands in Carbondale, Illinois, not in Kentucky. The web address stays under the Kentucky directory. The City of Carbondale's page for the Conductor Statue says the figure on the town square was placed there by Station Carbondale, Incorporated, through donations from people dedicated to the preservation of Carbondale's railroad history.

Fifty-three trains a day

The same city page says that at the peak of the city's railroad traffic, as many as 53 passenger trains passed through Carbondale each day. That is the measurement the city gives for the railroad years the statue marks.

How to use this address

The city page titles the work Conductor Statue and puts that town-square sentence in its account of Carbondale's railroad history. People looking for the figure are looking for the square in Carbondale, Illinois, where the city says donations from people who wanted that history remembered paid for the placement.
