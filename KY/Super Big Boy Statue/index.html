Super Big Boy in Metropolis

The Super Big Boy stands in Metropolis, Illinois. The page is filed under the Kentucky directory, and the web address stays there. The Super Museum's photo-op page calls it the one and only SUPER Big Boy Statue, a Superman-themed photo stop in front of Hidden Gems Novelty and Collectibles, painted by artist Morgan Siebert in 2020.

609 Market Street

The museum's Metropolis page says the statue is in front of Hidden Gems Novelty and Collectibles at 609 Market Street, and again credits the paint to Morgan Siebert. That is the street address this guide uses.

A different Superman on the square

The same town page describes another monument. On 5 June 1993, citizens of Metropolis unveiled a fifteen-foot bronze statue of Superman in front of the courthouse on Superman Square, before the town's fifteenth annual Superman Celebration. The museum says it was built by the company that created the Emmy statue outside the Academy of Television Arts in Hollywood. That bronze and the Super Big Boy are two objects. This URL is the Big Boy photo stop at 609 Market Street.

Questions

Where is the Super Big Boy?

The Super Museum places the Super Big Boy in front of Hidden Gems Novelty and Collectibles at 609 Market Street in Metropolis, Illinois. The figure is not in Kentucky. This guide keeps its Kentucky web address.

Is this the courthouse Superman?

No. The museum's town page says that on 5 June 1993 Metropolis unveiled a separate fifteen-foot bronze Superman in front of the courthouse on Superman Square, before the 15th annual Superman Celebration. The Super Big Boy is the photo stop painted by Morgan Siebert in 2020.
