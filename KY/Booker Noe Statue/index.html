The bronze the society describes

The Kentucky Brown Water Society journal note Booker and Dot, posted 30 November 2019, says that while visiting the Jim Beam American Still House the writers came across a bronze of Frederick Booker Noe II and his dog Dot. It says he was six foot four and a commanding figure even while sitting in his rocking chair. The six-foot-four figure is the note's description of Booker Noe himself.

The note says he was master distiller at Jim Beam for more than 40 years and filled Jim Beam's millionth barrel. It says that during a downturn, while clear spirits such as vodka and gin rose, he released small-batch bourbons that were unfiltered, uncut, and straight from the barrel, and that this barrel-proof method gave full flavor from the cask. It says Booker's bourbon won awards, including Wine Enthusiast's gold bourbon award, and that the success led to other labels, naming Baker's, Basil Hayden's, and Knob Creek.

The campus address and hours

Jim Beam's visit page says the James B. Beam Distilling Co. is in Clermont, Kentucky, on the Kentucky Bourbon Trail, and prints the address 568 Happy Hollow Road, Clermont, Kentucky 40110. It says the distillery is just south of Louisville. It says seven generations of the Beam family built the place and that bourbon has been made the Beam way since 1795. Those lines are the visit page's account of the distillery, not a plaque transcription from the bronze.

The same page lists distillery hours: Monday 9:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., Tuesday closed, Wednesday through Saturday 9:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., and Sunday 9:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. It says the campus is open through the week, with regular distillery hours and seasonal updates, and that opening times, restaurant hours, and tour availability may vary. Guests can visit the gift shop, restaurant, and outdoor spaces without a tour reservation. Excursions and tastings can sell out. Parking is on site.

The society note is the source for the bronze at the American Still House. The street address and hours are the campus facts on the visit page. The map pin is the distillery, at 37.9311218, -85.6525193, not a separate node for the rocking chair.

Questions

Where is the Booker Noe bronze?

The Kentucky Brown Water Society note places a bronze of Frederick Booker Noe II and his dog Dot at the Jim Beam American Still House. The visit page prints the campus address as 568 Happy Hollow Road, Clermont, Kentucky 40110.

Is six foot four the height of the statue?

The note uses six foot four for Booker Noe himself and says he was a commanding figure even while sitting in his rocking chair. It does not give a separate measurement of the bronze.

What hours does the visit page list?

Monday 9:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., Tuesday closed, Wednesday through Saturday 9:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., and Sunday 9:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., with a warning that seasonal updates may change tours and restaurants.
