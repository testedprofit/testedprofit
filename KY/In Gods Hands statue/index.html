In God's Hand Garden

Catholic Cemeteries of the Archdiocese of Louisville says Calvary Cemetery has a section for children of all ages. The Holy Innocents page says that in June 2010 Archbishop Joseph E. Kurtz dedicated the In God's Hand Garden. The spelling on that page is Hand, singular. This guide's URL says Hands, and the URL stays. The garden has a central bronze of God's hand cradling a baby, surrounded by over 1,500 granite pavers. The page says the pavers may be purchased to memorialize a child buried in Holy Innocents or in another section or cemetery.

The same page says families may buy a grave space in Holy Innocents for a level marker, including for fetuses and stillborn children, and that the burial is recorded. It also says Catholic Cemeteries can provide a free grave and burial in the section for fetuses and stillborns, that the exact location of that unmarked grave is not recorded, and that the grave cannot be marked. A prayer service is held at Holy Innocents on the last Wednesday of each month at 1:30 p.m. The names of children interred that month in the Catholic Cemeteries are mentioned, and other names can be mentioned at a family's request.

The Calvary page gives the address as 1600 Newburg Road, Louisville, Kentucky 40205. It says the cemetery was established in 1921, covers over 200 acres, and is the largest of the archdiocesan Catholic cemeteries. It says a Holy Innocents section is offered for infant and stillborn burials, and it mentions a monthly infant prayer service. These two pages do not print seasonal gate hours, so the older April-to-September and October-to-March clock times are not repeated. They also do not describe a separate statue of Rachel on the pages that opened, so that figure is not added.

Last updated 21 October 2026.

Questions

What is the sculpture called?

Catholic Cemeteries of Louisville says that in June 2010 Archbishop Joseph E. Kurtz dedicated the In God's Hand Garden. The garden has a central bronze of God's hand cradling a baby, surrounded by over 1,500 granite pavers. The URL of this guide says Hands. The cemetery page uses Hand.

Where is Calvary, and when is the prayer service?

The Calvary page gives 1600 Newburg Road, Louisville, Kentucky 40205, says the cemetery was established in 1921, and says it covers over 200 acres. A prayer service at Holy Innocents is held on the last Wednesday of each month at 1:30 p.m. Seasonal gate hours were not on these pages.
