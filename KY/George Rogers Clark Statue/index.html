George Rogers Clark statue

Louisville Downtown Partnership lists George Rogers Clark with the artist Felix W. de Weldon, the site Riverfront Plaza/Belvedere, and the sponsor line, a gift to the people of Louisville by the Hillman-Hopkins Family. The address on that listing is 485 West Main Street. The map on the same page uses a pin at latitude 38.258976 and longitude -85.75781. The listing does not print a medium. It does not describe a gesture, and it does not give a dedication day of its own.

Wikipedia's article on Riverfront Plaza/Belvedere says the project got 13.5 million dollars in funding in 1969 and was dedicated on 27 April 1973. It says the project runs between Third and Sixth streets, with a grassy park called the Belvedere on the western end. In the statues on that page, George Rogers Clark by Felix de Weldon is bronze and located immediately east of the park section. The same list includes a different bronze, York, by Ed Hamilton, erected in 2003 on the Belvedere. York is not the Clark statue.

Wikipedia's Felix de Weldon article lists a 1973 General George Rogers Clark in Louisville among his works. That list date and the 27 April 1973 plaza dedication are both on pages that opened. Neither page says the statue itself was unveiled at a named hour, so this guide does not turn the plaza date into a statue ceremony. A 1970 dedication and a 141 North Sixth Street address from older copy are not in these records and are not used. The downtown listing's medium line for marble or gold leaf was not on the page that opened, so those materials are not added. The bronze wording comes from the Belvedere article.

A different de Weldon list line, the 1973 library Franklin, is on the Benjamin Franklin guide.

Questions

Who made it, and where is the listing?

Louisville Downtown Partnership names the artist Felix W. de Weldon, the site Riverfront Plaza/Belvedere, the sponsor a gift of the Hillman-Hopkins Family, and the address 485 West Main Street. Wikipedia calls the sculpture bronze and places it immediately east of the Belvedere park section.

Was the statue dedicated in 1973?

Wikipedia says the Riverfront Plaza/Belvedere was dedicated on 27 April 1973, and its de Weldon article lists a 1973 General George Rogers Clark in Louisville. Those pages do not print a separate unveiling time for the statue. An older 1970 dedication line is not used.
