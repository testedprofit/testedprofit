Keith Whitley memorial

Wikipedia says Keith Whitley was born on 1 July 1954 in Ashland, Kentucky, to Faye Ferguson and Elmer Whitley, and was raised 46 miles away in Sandy Hook, where he attended Sandy Hook High School. The infobox lists his origin as Sandy Hook and says he died on 9 May 1989 in Goodlettsville, Tennessee, aged 34. He is buried in Spring Hill Cemetery outside Nashville. Wikipedia says he was born in 1954 according to his birth certificate and passport, and that his grave marker wrongly records 1955. It says he was inducted into the Country Music Hall of Fame in 2022. It says he and Ricky Skaggs, both 16, were discovered in Fort Gay, West Virginia, by Ralph Stanley.

The Kaintuckeean says Whitley was born in Ashland in 1954, with Elliott County roots back to the 1840s, and that in 1969 he became friends with Ricky Skaggs and the two were soon discovered by Ralph Stanley and J.D. Crowe. Wikipedia says Ralph Stanley discovered Whitley and Skaggs in Fort Gay, West Virginia. The tribute says he released five consecutive number-one singles, including When You Say Nothing at All and I'm No Stranger to the Rain. It says he collapsed in 1989 at age 33 with a blood alcohol level of .477. Wikipedia says the official cause was acute ethanol poisoning and quotes medical examiner Charles Harlan that the level was 0.47. The infobox age is 34 and the tribute's age is 33. The tribute says he is buried at Nashville's Spring Hill Cemetery.

On the statue, the Kaintuckeean says the Keith Whitley Memorial Association commissioned Montana sculptor Bill Rains to portray Whitley with his guitar, and that the sculpture has been displayed at the cemetery in Sandy Hook since 1999. The Kentucky Music Trail venue page also places a Keith Whitley statue in Sandy Hook. The tribute is the page that names Bill Rains, the guitar, and the year 1999.

Questions

Who carved the Sandy Hook statue, and when was it displayed?

The Kaintuckeean says the Keith Whitley Memorial Association commissioned Montana sculptor Bill Rains, that the figure has a guitar, and that it has been displayed at the cemetery in Sandy Hook since 1999. The Kentucky Music Trail also places a Keith Whitley statue in Sandy Hook.

When was Keith Whitley born and when did he die?

Wikipedia says he was born 1 July 1954 in Ashland, raised in Sandy Hook, and died 9 May 1989 in Goodlettsville, Tennessee. Its infobox says aged 34. The Kaintuckeean says age 33. Wikipedia says his blood alcohol level was 0.47. The tribute says .477. His grave marker's 1955 birth year is called an error.
