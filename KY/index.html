Kentucky landmark guides

This page is the index of Kentucky landmark guides on TestedProfit. Twenty guides are filed under the Kentucky directory, and each card uses the title of the page it opens. Earning-app guides and the national park archive are linked from the related list, separate from these cards. The Kansas directory is linked there as the previous state section.

Two cards use titles that name Carbondale, Illinois, and Metropolis, Illinois. Those are the train conductor in Carbondale and the Super Big Boy in Metropolis, and both stay at their Kentucky web addresses. The other cards use the titles of the Kentucky guides they open, from Benjamin Franklin through the Wooldridge Monuments.
