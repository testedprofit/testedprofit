Chess carving

This guide keeps its existing URL. On 21 October 2026 the WDRB news address that search results still title as a Mount Washington tree carving redirected to the station homepage at wdrbwave.com. The response was that homepage, not the article. A check of the Internet Archive's capture list for the same article address returned no saved page with status 200. Because the story text did not load, this page does not repeat a carver's name, a homeowner's name, a count of days, a height, a street address, a zip code, or a claim that the carving is a chess king or a chess knight.

The folder name on this site includes the words Chess King Statue Carving. That string is part of the URL, which stays so existing links keep working. It is not treated as a verified identification of the subject. Older marketing copy on this URL added a private-yard etiquette note and a measurement. Those sentences are removed until a page that actually returns the story can be read.

The station homepage that did open is linked below and labeled as the redirect target, not as an article about the carving. No other local page for this URL returned a usable account on this pass. Readers who want the subject, the maker, and the town named should wait for a later check if that news story opens, or follow a report whose full text returns. This guide will not fill the gap with the search-result snippet.

Other Kentucky guides in this batch were rewritten from pages that did open, including the Armco Sasquatch, Ozzie Frank at Rooster Run, and the Belvedere's George Rogers Clark. Those sculptures are separate. Nothing in their records describes this carving, and their facts are not borrowed to pad this page.
