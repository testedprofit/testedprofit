Chess sculpture carving in Kentucky

This guide is the Kentucky landmark page for a chess sculpture carving. It is filed with the other sculpture guides in the Kentucky directory, and the directory card uses the title of this page. The web address stays the same, so an existing link to the guide still opens it.

The Kentucky directory is the index for this page and for the other guides filed under the state. Those guides include Benjamin Franklin at the Louisville Public Library, the Sasquatch carving at Armco Park in Ashland, Booker Noe and Dot at the Jim Beam campus in Clermont, and Ozzie Frank at Rooster Run in Nelson County. Daniel Boone stands in the Cherokee Park listing, George Rogers Clark stands at the Belvedere on West Main Street, and the In God's Hand Garden is at Calvary Cemetery. The Thomas Jefferson monument is at Metro Hall, and the Keith Whitley memorial is in Sandy Hook. Each of those sculptures has its own guide.

The same directory also lists Little Elina, the Railroad Memorial, Secretariat, Squire Boone, Abraham Lincoln, Charlie Farnsley, Super Big Boy, the Cutting Edge of Simpson County, the Statue of David, and the Wooldridge Monuments. Two of those titles name Carbondale, Illinois, and Metropolis, Illinois, and the guides remain at their Kentucky addresses. Use the directory to move from this carving to any of those pages.

A citation to this guide should use its own address, https://testedprofit.com/KY/Harry%20Potter%20Chess%20King%20Statue%20Carving/. The Kentucky directory is the page that lists it beside the rest of the state's landmark guides.
