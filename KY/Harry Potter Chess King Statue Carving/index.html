Harry Potter chess king statue carving

The Harry Potter Chess King Statue Carving is a roadside landmark in Mt. Washington, Kentucky. Travelers find the carving in that Bullitt County city.

Wikipedia says Mount Washington is a city in northeast Bullitt County, one of the communities around Louisville that grew into a commuter town. The population was 18,090 at the 2020 census. The city sits at what was once the crossroads of two stagecoach routes and was originally known as The Cross Roads. It was formally established in 1822 as Mount Vernon, after George Washington's Virginia home. Another town's post office already had that name, and at the city's first formal incorporation in 1833 the name became Mount Washington, again in honor of the first president. By the 1850s it was the economic center of Bullitt County. Wikipedia says that in the autumn of 1862, during the Civil War, John Wharton's Confederate cavalry held the town and skirmished with Maj. Gen. Thomas L. Crittenden's men north of Mount Washington along Floyds Fork on October 1.

The Harry Potter Chess King Statue Carving is the roadside stop in that city, a Bullitt County crossroads that has been Mount Washington since 1833, within reach of Louisville.
