Muffler Man Big Red

Big Red on Dupont Road

Muffler Man Big Red is a roadside landmark in Parkersburg. Wikipedia's list of muffler men says Big Red stands in Parkersburg near the South Parkersburg Shopping Plaza, that it was erected in the early 1980s, and that it has served as a landmark for Cherokee Village Auto Center and other businesses at its location. A Parkersburg guide published August 28, 2018 calls the figure an Indian Muffler Man and says it stands over the Cherokee Village Auto Center Title and License Service Center. That guide gives the address as 1704 Dupont Rd., Parkersburg, West Virginia 26101.

The same guide says muffler men are fiberglass and that repairs are costly. The photograph on that page is captioned The Muffler Man in Parkersburg. The list of muffler men and the 2018 guide are the two location lines for this statue: near the South Parkersburg Shopping Plaza, and 1704 Dupont Rd. at Cherokee Village.

The fiberglass figures

Wikipedia's Muffler Man article says muffler men are large molded fiberglass sculptures placed as advertising icons, roadside attractions, or for decorative purposes, predominantly in the United States. It says they stand approximately 18 to 25 feet tall. That range describes the figures as a group. International Fiberglass of Venice, California constructed most muffler men. Boatbuilder Steve Dashew established International Fiberglass in 1963. The company closed permanently in 1976. The list of muffler men dates the erection of Big Red in Parkersburg to the early 1980s, after that closing date.

The article says the oversized figures began as a sideline, and that molds let makers swap heads, limbs, or torsos. Thousands were deployed in a little over a decade, often customized for roadside businesses. The Parkersburg figure is the one the list names Big Red and the 2018 guide calls an Indian Muffler Man at Cherokee Village.

Questions

Where is the Parkersburg muffler man?

A 2018 Parkersburg guide places the Indian Muffler Man at 1704 Dupont Rd., Parkersburg, West Virginia 26101, at the Cherokee Village Auto Center Title and License Service Center. Wikipedia's list of muffler men calls the figure Big Red and says it was erected in the early 1980s near the South Parkersburg Shopping Plaza as a landmark for Cherokee Village Auto Center.

What is a muffler man?

Wikipedia says muffler men are large molded fiberglass sculptures used as advertising icons, roadside attractions, or decoration, and that the figures stand approximately 18 to 25 feet. International Fiberglass of Venice, California constructed most of them. That company closed in 1976. The height range is the article's figure for muffler men as a group.
