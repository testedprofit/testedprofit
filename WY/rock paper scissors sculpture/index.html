Rock Paper Scissors SculptureGillette, Wyoming: how to see it

A street-corner sculpture of the world's favorite tie-breaker game by Kevin Box and Warren Cullar, stacked rock, paper, then scissors on top. Spoiler: scissors wins. It's free, it's outdoors, and it takes five minutes off US-16.

The three pieces of the game are stacked into one tower, with the rock below, the paper in the middle and a pair of scissors balanced on top. Kevin Box, the co-artist, is known for origami-inspired sculpture cast in metal. His studio describes the game as "a way of making decisions or resolving conflicts for not so serious things." The joke is that serious matters deserve real conversation.

The sculpture is part of Gillette's Avenues of Art, a Mayor's Art Council program. It places sculptures along city streets and uses a share of sales to add pieces to the city's permanent collection.

Visiting tips

  • Find it fast: look for the News-Record building at W 2nd St & S Burma Ave. US-16 runs through this stretch of 2nd St.
  • Best photo: take it from the sidewalk, and throw your own "rock, paper or scissors" next to the stack.
  • Pair it with: the Rockpile Museum (900 W 2nd St), less than a mile east on the same street.
  • Time needed: about 5–10 minutes, which makes it an easy stop on a Wyoming road trip.

Rock Paper Scissors sculpture FAQ

Where is the Rock Paper Scissors sculpture in Gillette?

At 1201 W 2nd St, Gillette, WY 82716, on the southwest corner of S. Burma Ave. and W. 2nd St. (US Hwy 16), in front of the Gillette News-Record offices.

Who made the Rock Paper Scissors sculpture?

Artists Kevin Box and Warren Cullar. It was installed in 2011 as part of Gillette's Avenues of Art public-art program.

Does it cost anything to see?

No. It is outdoor public art on a street corner, free to view at any time.

So who wins: rock, paper or scissors?

Scissors. The pieces are stacked with the rock at the base, the paper in the middle and the scissors on top.

What else is nearby?

The Rockpile Museum, a county history museum at 900 W 2nd St, is less than a mile east on the same street.
