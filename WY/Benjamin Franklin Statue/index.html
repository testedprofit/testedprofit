Benjamin Franklin statue

South of the College of Arts and Science

The Benjamin Franklin statue is a campus landmark in Laramie. The Branding Iron, in a story published April 11, 2023, says it sits south of the College of Arts and Science building and is one of the most prominent statues on the University of Wyoming campus. A University of Wyoming Art Museum list for the sculpture tour Sculpture: A Wyoming Invitational names the work Ben Franklin, by Robert I. Russin, and places it south of the College of A&S.

The Branding Iron says Russin created the statue in 1956. It is bronze. It was dedicated to Franklin as a gift for his 250th birthday. The same story says Russin also designed the University Family and the Abraham Lincoln statue off Interstate 80. It says this Franklin is one of four statues in the world that show him standing.

The Branding Iron prints the plaque. It calls him a great American statesman, philosopher, scientist, man of letters, and civic leader, and says his accomplishments have enriched his homeland and the world. The story says the plaque goes on to list Richard's Almanack, the American Philosophical Society, the academy that became the University of Pennsylvania, the Continental Congress, the committee for the Declaration of Independence, Postmaster General, minister to France, the Treaty of Paris, and the autobiography. The line at the bottom of the plaque reads, Where liberty dwells, there is my country. The story says Franklin was born in Boston in 1706 and died in 1790 at age 84.

Questions

Where is the Benjamin Franklin statue?

The Branding Iron places it south of the College of Arts and Science building on the University of Wyoming campus in Laramie. A University of Wyoming Art Museum sculpture-tour list also places Robert I. Russin's Ben Franklin south of the College of A&S.

Who made it, and when?

The Branding Iron says Robert Russin created the bronze in 1956 and that it was dedicated as a gift for Franklin's 250th birthday. The same story says it is one of four statues in the world that show Franklin standing.
