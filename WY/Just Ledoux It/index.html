Just LeDoux It

A bronze at Frontier Park

Just LeDoux It is the Chris LeDoux memorial at Frontier Park in Cheyenne. The Cheyenne Frontier Days Foundation titles the work Just LeDoux It and says Wyoming musician and world-champion bareback rider Chris LeDoux was a veteran of the Cheyenne Frontier Days rodeo arena by day and the main stage at night. The statue depicts his two great loves, rodeo and music, and pays tribute to the career he built around the Western way of life.

The Foundation says Wyoming artist D. Michael Thomas created the Just LeDoux It memorial bronze. It was dedicated in 2021, on opening day of the 125th Cheyenne Frontier Days celebration, the same year the event was dedicated to LeDoux. Today it stands on the west side of Frontier Park, facing Hynds Boulevard.

The sculptor's own page titles the piece Just LeDoux It and calls it a tribute to Chris LeDoux for Cheyenne Frontier Days, with a 2020 copyright. It lists the scale as Life and 1/2+ and the medium as bronze. The sculptor's page carries a 2020 copyright. The Foundation dates the dedication to 2021. Thomas's note on the page says he was glad they were honoring LeDoux for the 125th Daddy of 'em All, and that LeDoux went to high school in Cheyenne, rode, and performed as a headliner.

What is Just LeDoux It?

The Cheyenne Frontier Days Foundation calls it the Just LeDoux It memorial bronze of Chris LeDoux, made by Wyoming artist D. Michael Thomas. It was dedicated in 2021, on opening day of the 125th Cheyenne Frontier Days, the year the event was dedicated to LeDoux. The sculptor's page titles the work Just LeDoux It, a tribute for Cheyenne Frontier Days, with a 2020 copyright and a scale listed as Life and 1/2+.

Where does the statue stand?

The Foundation says it stands on the west side of Frontier Park, facing Hynds Boulevard. LeDoux was a world-champion bareback rider and a Wyoming musician who rode in the Frontier Days arena and played the main stage.
