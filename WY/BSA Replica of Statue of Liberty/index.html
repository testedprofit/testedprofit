BSA Statue of Liberty replica

A Boy Scout replica in Cheyenne

The BSA Statue of Liberty replica is the Cheyenne figure from the Boy Scouts' Strengthen the Arm of Liberty campaign. Wikipedia says the Boy Scouts of America marked their fortieth anniversary in 1950 with that theme. Between 1949 and 1952, approximately two hundred 100-inch replicas, made of stamped copper, were purchased by troops and donated in 39 states and several territories. Kansas City businessman J.P. Whitaker, then Scout Commissioner of the Kansas City Area Council, is credited with the project. Friedley-Voshardt Co. of Chicago manufactured the copper statues, which were bought through the Kansas City Boy Scout office.

Wikipedia also says the statues are approximately 8.5 feet tall without the base, constructed of sheet copper, weigh 290 pounds, and originally cost 350 dollars plus freight. It says three of the Boy Scout statues are in Wyoming: in Lions Park in Cheyenne, at the Carbon County Courthouse in Rawlins, and at the Goshen County District Courthouse in Torrington. The 100-inch figure and the 8.5-foot figure are both on that page.

Capitol grounds, the Supreme Court, and Lions Park

An April 11, 2008 story says Leland Duck and his father, Ron, began work on the eight-foot-tall statue as Leland's Eagle Scout project. It says the statue formerly stood on the Capitol grounds and later at the state Supreme Court. The copper figure had been dented, and the prongs on the head had been destroyed, so they had a bronze artist make molds and recast it. The story says the copper one weighed about 60 pounds and the bronze one 400 pounds. Leland raised about 2,500 dollars, and the city contributed 6,000 dollars. The story describes a plan to feature the bronze as the centerpiece of Law and Liberty Square in north Lions Park, across Lions Park Drive and northeast of the municipal pool. Rick Parish, director of the Cheyenne Division of Parks and Recreation, is quoted on the Boy Scouts' help. The 2008 story's weights are about 60 pounds for the copper statue and 400 pounds for the bronze. Wikipedia's figure for the copper series is 290 pounds. The 2008 story says eight feet. Wikipedia says about 100 inches and about 8.5 feet.

Questions

Where is Cheyenne's BSA Statue of Liberty replica?

Wikipedia's list of Statue of Liberty replicas says three Boy Scout statues are in Wyoming, including one in Lions Park in Cheyenne. An April 11, 2008 story says this statue formerly stood on the Capitol grounds and later at the state Supreme Court, and describes a plan to place a bronze recast in Law and Liberty Square in north Lions Park.

How tall is it, and what does it weigh?

Wikipedia says the Boy Scout replicas are about 100 inches, and also about 8.5 feet without the base, and that the sheet-copper figures weigh 290 pounds. The April 11, 2008 story calls the Cheyenne statue eight feet tall, says the copper one weighed about 60 pounds, and says the bronze recast weighs 400 pounds.
