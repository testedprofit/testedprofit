Big Boy statue

A Big Boy on the road to Yellowstone

The Big Boy statue is the roadside figure in Wapiti. Cowboy State Daily, on June 11, 2023, says anyone driving to or from Yellowstone National Park's east entrance on U.S. Highway 14/16/20 over the previous decade was likely to have seen the Wapiti Big Boy. It describes a larger-than-life statue on a concrete pedestal in the middle of a field about 20 miles west of Cody, with a smile, serving a giant burger. The story says it is an authentic Big Boy rescued from a California Big Boy restaurant, and that it was placed in that field on purpose.

James Geier told Cowboy State Daily that he owns the statue and the land. He said Big Boy restaurants were everywhere at one time and that he had always wanted one. He said he had heard people claim it just showed up. The story says he put it there, deliberately, in 2013.

The Cody Enterprise of October 12, 2026 says the Big Boy statue returned to his pedestal along the south side of the North Fork Highway in Wapiti on the previous Saturday night. The story says that return ended a short stay by a Captain Morgan figure. It says that for about 13 years, drivers bound for Yellowstone had passed the grinning kid in checkered overalls holding a double-decker burger over his head. It says the figure vanished that fall and a pirate with a sword and a barrel appeared in its place. Tim Whitmore, described as a longtime collaborator of property owner James Geier, said the sheriff's office called to ask whether the statue had been vandalized, and that the captain was only keeping Big Boy's seat hot. Geier dates the placement to 2013. The Enterprise counts about 13 years of drivers passing the figure.

Last updated 21 October 2026.

Questions

Where is the Wapiti Big Boy?

Cowboy State Daily says the statue stands on a concrete pedestal in a field about 20 miles west of Cody along U.S. Highway 14/16/20, on the way to Yellowstone's east entrance. The Cody Enterprise of October 12, 2026 says it stands on the south side of the North Fork Highway in Wapiti.

Who put it there?

James Geier told Cowboy State Daily that he owns the statue and the land and that he put it there in 2013. The story says it is an authentic Big Boy from a California Big Boy restaurant. The Cody Enterprise says the figure returned to the pedestal on the Saturday night before its October 12, 2026 story, after a Captain Morgan figure had stood in its place.
