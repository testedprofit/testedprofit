Robert Burns memorial statue

Gilchrist Park

The Robert Burns memorial statue is the bronze of Scotland's poet in Cheyenne. Wikipedia's list of Robert Burns memorials says a bronze statue was erected in Gilchrist Park in 1929 by Scottish sculptor Henry Snell Gamley, who lived from 1865 to 1928. The Wyoming State Archives finding aid for the Robert Burns statue records says the memorial was erected in Cheyenne and dedicated on November 11, 1929, and that Mary Gilchrist funded the project.

The finding aid says Mary Gemmell Gilchrist was born in 1840 in Scotland, emigrated in 1869, and settled in Cheyenne in 1875 with her husband Andrew. Andrew died in 1894. Mary remained in Cheyenne and gave the statue to the city in honor of her native Scotland. The archives say the statue stands in the small triangular park at the intersections of Randall Avenue, Pioneer Avenue, and 26th Street. She died in January 1931 and is buried with Andrew in Lakeview Cemetery.

Wikipedia's biography of Gamley says that in 1928 he hired Auguste Rodin's studio in Paris to work on a statue of Robert Burns for export to Wyoming, and that he died that year on a country estate near Paris. Gamley died in 1928. The archives date the dedication to November 11, 1929, and Wikipedia dates the erection in Gilchrist Park to 1929.

Questions

Where is the Robert Burns statue?

Wikipedia's list of Robert Burns memorials says a bronze statue was erected in Gilchrist Park in Cheyenne in 1929 by Henry Snell Gamley. The Wyoming State Archives finding aid says the statue stands in the small triangular park at Randall Avenue, Pioneer Avenue, and 26th Street.

Who paid for it, and when was it dedicated?

The finding aid says Mary Gilchrist funded the statue and that it was dedicated on November 11, 1929. She gave it to the City of Cheyenne in honor of her native Scotland. Wikipedia says Gamley lived from 1865 to 1928, and his biography says that in 1928 he worked in Auguste Rodin's Paris studio on a Burns statue for export to Wyoming.
