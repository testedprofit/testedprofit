Larry Bird's childhood home

Larry Bird's childhood home is a roadside landmark in French Lick, Indiana. French Lick is the town where he grew up.

Wikipedia says Larry Joe Bird was born on 7 December 1956 in West Baden Springs, Indiana, to Georgia Marie Kerns and Claude Joseph Bird, called Joe, a veteran of the Second World War and the Korean War. He has four brothers and a sister. The article says he was raised in nearby French Lick, where his mother worked two jobs to support Larry and his five siblings. His parents divorced while he was in high school, and Joe died by suicide in February 1975. Wikipedia says Bird used basketball as an escape from those family troubles.

The same article says he starred at Springs Valley High School in French Lick, class of 1974, and as a senior averaged 31 points, 21 rebounds, and 4.0 assists on the way to becoming the school's all-time scoring leader. He wore number 33 in high school because his brother Mark had worn it, and he kept that number in college and in the NBA. The lead calls him the Hick from French Lick. He played his whole 13-season career with the Boston Celtics, with three NBA championships, in 1981, 1984, and 1986, and three Most Valuable Player awards from 1984 through 1986. He was inducted into the Naismith Memorial Basketball Hall of Fame in 1998. The childhood home in French Lick is the roadside landmark. West Baden Springs is the birthplace Wikipedia names beside it.

A wooden landmark in the same area is Otis at French Lick Cabins.
