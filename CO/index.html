Colorado guides

This page is the index of Colorado guides on TestedProfit. The lists below cover the landmark guides filed in the Colorado directory, from Big Baldy in Dillon through Westminster City Park, plus the bear guide those pages point to. A further list links other Colorado pages on this site, including Denver and Vail guides and park pages outside the /CO/ folder.

Cards are titles only. Each card uses the title of the page it opens. There is no blurb under a card. The address /CO/Landmarks/Bear-Statue/ is a redirect to /pages/bear-statue/ and is not listed as a separate guide. The address /pages/james-dalton-trumbo-statue/ now redirects to /CO/James%20Dalton%20Trumbo%20Statue/ and is not listed as a second Trumbo guide. Redirect stubs for Woodbury Park, rotary park, and the Cedar Mountain local guide are not listed either. Earning-app guides stay in their own section and are linked from the related list.

Arizona and Wyoming guides are not mixed into this Colorado index. The national landmarks hub and the national park archive stay in the related list because they cover more than one state.
