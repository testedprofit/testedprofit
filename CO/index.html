Colorado landmark guides

This page is the index of Colorado landmark guides on TestedProfit. It covers the guides filed under the Colorado directory. Nine of those guides were rewritten on 21 October 2026 from public records, and their cards use the new titles. The bear card opens the existing guide at /pages/bear-statue/. The address /CO/Landmarks/Bear-Statue/ is a redirect to that keeper and is not listed as a separate guide. The second group still uses the current titles of pages that have not been revised yet.

Cards are titles only. There is no blurb under a card, because a blurb would repeat claims that later pages have not been checked. When a later pass revises one of those guides, the card title should be replaced with the new title. Earning-app guides are a separate section and are linked from the related list, not mixed into these cards.

Statue guides that already stand on their own URLs outside this directory were not linked from the old Colorado index. They are not added here. This index stays with the Colorado directory pages and the bear keeper that the old bear card was already trying to reach.

Questions

What does this directory include?

It lists the Colorado landmark guides published on TestedProfit. Nine guides in the first group were revised on 21 October 2026. The bear card opens the existing guide at its keeper URL. The remaining cards still use the titles of pages that have not been revised yet.

Do the cards match the guides?

Each card uses the title of the page it opens. Revised guides use their new titles. Pages left for a later pass keep the titles they have now.
