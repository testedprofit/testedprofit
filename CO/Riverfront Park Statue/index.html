Riverfront Park

The park the tourism site lists

Visit Durango's listing for Riverfront Park gives the address 1295 Camino del Rio, Durango, CO 81301. It lists three features: the Animas River Trail, a fishing area, and a grass area. The directions say to leave Main Avenue west on 9th Street, turn north on Camino del Rio, go about a quarter mile, and turn left one block past Veterans Way. The road turns right, and the park is on the left. That listing does not name a sculpture, an artist, or a metal figure.

The trail, and the address this page used to give

The City of Durango trails page says the Animas River Trail is a hard-surface shared-use path and the centerpiece of the city's trail system. It stretches nearly 7 miles through the Animas River Greenway and connects parks, open space, the recreation center, the library, downtown, neighborhoods, and schools. A 2019 city trail brochure measures one span separately: from the trail terminus next to Dallabetta Park, near the Rivera Bridge, to Animas City Park is approximately 7.5 miles. Those are the two distances in the records that loaded. The older page on this URL placed an unnamed metal statue at 1129 Camino del Rio and added a 24-hour schedule and a wildlife list. The tourism listing's park address is 1295 Camino del Rio, and neither that listing nor the city trail page names a statue at 1129. This guide does not keep an artist, a material, or a round-the-clock claim for a sculpture those pages do not identify.

The URL of this guide still says statue, because the address is kept. The visit it can support from the pages that loaded is the park and the trail, not a named artwork. If a later city art record names a piece at the park, the card title can change with it.

Questions

Where is Riverfront Park?

Visit Durango lists Riverfront Park at 1295 Camino del Rio, Durango, CO 81301, with the Animas River Trail, a fishing area, and a grass area. It does not name a sculpture.

How long is the Animas River Trail?

The city trails page says the trail stretches nearly 7 miles through the Animas River Greenway. A 2019 city brochure says the distance from Dallabetta Park near the Rivera Bridge to Animas City Park is approximately 7.5 miles.
