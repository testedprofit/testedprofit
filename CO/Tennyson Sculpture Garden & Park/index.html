Tennyson Sculpture Garden

475 Lewis Street

Two Old Crows, the gallery across the street, describes the Tennyson Sculpture Park and Gardens at 475 Lewis Street in Pagosa Springs. It says the park has shown contemporary sculpture since opening in 2023, that ten pieces are on permanent display, that there is no admission fee, and that it is open nine months of the year and closed in the winter. Visit Pagosa Springs lists the same garden at 475 Lewis St #104, Pagosa Springs, CO 81147. The gallery's own street address on that page is 468 Lewis Street. The Pagosa Daily Post says Two Old Crows stands across the street from the park.

The ribbon cutting and the 2023 date

The Pagosa Springs Sun, posted 26 June 2024, reports the grand opening of the Tennyson Sculpture Park and Gardens on Lewis Street on 22 June, with Evelyn Tennyson and the artists speaking and the family cutting a ribbon. The Daily Post dates that Saturday to 22 June 2024, at about 11 a.m., and says a crowd of perhaps 200 came for the ribbon cutting. Evelyn Tennyson told that crowd the park is a memorial to Michael Tennyson, that the Adobe building and the Bear Creek Saloon burned, she thought in 2016, and that buying the lot took about four years. She said Mike died in 2017 and that the park was her way of honoring him and the town, and that sculpture was her interest rather than his. The Daily Post's writer says he does not believe public funding was involved. The gallery page says the park opened in 2023. The Sun and the Daily Post date the public ribbon cutting to 22 June 2024. Both dates stay on this page.

Works the gallery names

The gallery page names pieces on the grounds, including Native by Dave Lamure Jr., Wind Eagle by Dustin Miller, Seasons' Shed by James Ferrari, Blue Sun by Chad Haspels, and Harmony by Ross Barrable, which it describes as a garden bell. A note signed by Evelyn Tennyson dedicates the park, the gardens, and the sculptures to friends and family, and especially to her husband Michael Duane Tennyson, 1952 to 2017. The older page's wheelchair line and a gong product name are not in these three pages, so they are not repeated. The gallery hours printed for Two Old Crows, Tuesday through Friday 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. and Saturday 10 a.m. to 3 p.m., are the gallery's hours, not a posted gate time for the park.

Last updated 21 October 2026.

Questions

Where is the Tennyson Sculpture Garden, and when did it open?

The gallery places it at 475 Lewis Street. Visit Pagosa Springs adds suite 104 and the zip code 81147. The gallery says it opened in 2023. The Pagosa Springs Sun and the Pagosa Daily Post date the ribbon cutting to 22 June 2024.

Who is it for?

Evelyn Tennyson's note dedicates the park to Michael Duane Tennyson, 1952 to 2017. She told the Daily Post crowd that he died in 2017 and that the garden is a memorial park.
