Desert Metal Art

A shop, not a monument

Desert Metal Art is a retail metal-sculpture business in Grand Junction. It is not a city-owned statue. The Business Times, in an article dated 3 June 2026, reported that John and Jennifer Gallegos own and operate the shop at 929 Pitkin Avenue, the address where they previously ran the used-car dealership Desert Auto. John Gallegos told the paper they had been in the metal-art business three years and that it had replaced the car business.

The same article describes the lot as a display of rusted-metal animals, including a pair of bucking horses, a bull, and a gorilla. It says a tall rusty Bigfoot figure and a velociraptor stand near the parking-lot entrance. It also quotes customers choosing metal flowers, metal cactus, and other metal pieces instead of live plants. The older page on this URL listed a much longer wildlife inventory, retail hours, and a spot across from a skate park. Those details are not in the Business Times article, so they are not repeated.

Two street numbers

A trade-name listing on OpenGovCO, which says its records come from Colorado Department of State filings, lists the trade name Desert Metal Art for registrant Jennifer Mariah Gallegos at 919 Pitkin Avenue, Grand Junction, CO 81501, effective 10 May 2023. The Secretary of State detail page did not return a readable record on this check, so this guide does not link that office and does not choose between 919 and 929 Pitkin Avenue. Both numbers are left on the page because both sources state them.

Last updated 21 October 2026.

Questions

Is Desert Metal Art a public statue?

No. The Business Times describes it as a metal-art shop at the former Desert Auto lot, owned by John and Jennifer Gallegos. The yard displays are merchandise.

Which Pitkin Avenue address is listed?

The Business Times, on 3 June 2026, places the shop at 929 Pitkin Avenue. An OpenGovCO trade-name listing drawn from Colorado Department of State filings gives 919 Pitkin Avenue, effective 10 May 2023. This guide does not pick one number.
