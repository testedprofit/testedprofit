Capt. William H. DuBois Jr. memorial

Grand River Park

The memorial to Capt. William H. DuBois Jr., known as Will and as Pyro, stands in Grand River Park in New Castle. The Post Independent reported the dedication on 10 September 2016. A photo caption in that story places the Captain William H. DuBois, Jr. memorial dedication at Grand River Park on that date. The same paper's unveiling story, also dated 10 September 2016, says the ceremony started at noon in the park and calls the work a roughly 8-foot-tall bronze by John Kobald, a sculptor based in Meeker. The dedication story describes the finished face: the sculptor recommended a more reserved expression, and the family could not find a photograph without Will's smile. Ham DuBois, Will's father, told that to the crowd.

The pilot

The Pyro's Wings foundation page says Will Pyro DuBois was born on 14 August 1984 in the New Castle area, graduated from Rifle High School, and studied at the University of Colorado in Boulder. It says his first assignment, in 2011, was Kunsan, where he flew for the 35th Fighter Squadron, the Pantons, and earned the call sign Pyro. It says he later flew at Shaw Air Force Base with the 77th Fighter Squadron, the Gamblers, and that the unit deployed in October in support of Inherent Resolve from an unnamed base. It says his wingman had a problem after takeoff and they were trying to return when he crashed on 1 December 2014. The Post Independent dedication story says he died on 1 December 2014 when his F-16 crashed in Jordan on a mission against ISIS, his 19th such mission since a deployment in October 2014. The same story says the nickname Pyro was earned during pilot school. This guide keeps the foundation's Kunsan call-sign account and the dedication story's pilot-school account, and it keeps both the unnamed base and Jordan.

What the town raised

The dedication story says friends and community leaders expected to need $120,000. Donated items brought that cost down. After raising $117,000, roughly $5,000 was to go to the Pyro's Wings Foundation, the scholarship fund the family created for future combat pilots. The unveiling story says the goal was $120,000 and that supporters raised around $5,000 more than the sculpture required, with John Lee telling the paper that money was still coming in. Both figures are left as the two articles state them.

Questions

Where is the Pyro DuBois memorial, and when was it dedicated?

The Post Independent places the dedication of the Captain William H. DuBois, Jr. memorial at Grand River Park in New Castle on 10 September 2016. Its unveiling story calls the work a roughly 8-foot bronze by John Kobald of Meeker.

When did Capt. DuBois die, and where do the accounts place the crash?

Both the Post Independent and the Pyro's Wings page date his death to 1 December 2014. The Post Independent says his F-16 crashed in Jordan. The foundation page says the unit was flying from an unnamed base and that the crash came while he and his wingman were returning after a mechanical problem.
