Westminster City Park

205 acres at 10455 Sheridan

The City of Westminster's facility page lists City Park at 10455 Sheridan Boulevard, Westminster, CO 80031, and gives the size as 205 acres. It classifies the park as regional or citywide. The amenity list names access to Big Dry Creek and North Hylands Creek open space, the Big Dry Creek regional trail, the Armed Forces Tribute Garden, Christopher Fields with four softball fields, four soccer fields, a drinking fountain, a fitness center, parking, a paved loop path with climbing stairs, three pavilions of which one is reservable, nineteen picnic tables, playground equipment, a pond where fishing is not allowed, the recreation center, restrooms, a skateboard park, and trails. The page does not post a general open-or-close hour for the grounds. It does post water-feature hours.

The fountain and the tribute garden

The park page labels the water-feature hours as closed for drought restrictions, and it also says the feature is open from Memorial Day through Labor Day, with a note that the fountains may be shut off for weather, climate, maintenance, or other operations. Both lines are on the page, so both are repeated here. The city's Armed Forces Tribute Garden page places that garden just east of U.S. 36 on 104th Avenue, between Christopher Fields and Big Dry Creek. It says the garden honors the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, Coast Guard, and Merchant Marines, and that it has a central water feature, seating, granite pillars, and walkways with engraved pavers. The fountain season on that page is the same Memorial Day to Labor Day window. The page does not give a count of pavers.

Disc golf

The city's calendar lists a disc-golf grand reopening on Saturday, 25 October 2025, from 11:45 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., at 10495 Sheridan Boulevard, after the course was temporarily removed for a construction project. That event address is not the same number as the park's 10455 Sheridan Boulevard. A UDisc course page says the course was established in 2003, closed in 2023, and redesigned in 2025, and that the new layout has nine holes as the first of two phases toward 18, with short and long positions on most holes. Those hole counts and years are the directory's, not a line on the city park page.

Questions

How big is Westminster City Park, and what is the address?

The city facility page says 205 acres at 10455 Sheridan Boulevard, Westminster, CO 80031. It lists four softball fields at Christopher Fields, four soccer fields, three pavilions, nineteen picnic tables, and a pond where fishing is not allowed.

When did the disc-golf course reopen?

The city calendar sets a grand reopening for 25 October 2025 at 10495 Sheridan Boulevard, after a construction removal. A UDisc page says the course was established in 2003, closed in 2023, redesigned in 2025, and now has nine holes as the first phase toward 18.
