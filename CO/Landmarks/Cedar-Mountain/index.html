Cedar Mountain

The BLM area

The Bureau of Land Management describes Cedar Mountain as a Special Recreation Management Area of 880 acres. The page calls it a horseshoe-shaped, juniper-rimmed mountain standing 1,000 feet above the Yampa Valley, isolated enough that the rim looks out over the surrounding country. The coordinates on the page are 40.57504, -107.61831. The area is day-use only. The fee line says it is a free area. Amenities listed are a parking area, a pit toilet, and individual and group picnic tables with fire grates. Motorized vehicles are limited to signed, designated roads.

The BLM page lists year-round activities: wildlife viewing, nature study, picnicking, hiking, mountain biking, cross-country skiing, snowshoeing, and horseback riding. The 3.5-mile Stearns Trail starts from the parking area and is used on foot, on horseback, and by mountain bike. A separate half-mile hiking trail begins at the covered picnic tables. An older URL, /pages/cedar-mountain-craig-co-local-guide/, already redirects to this page, so this URL remains the guide.

Rules and ground

Hunting is allowed during hunting season only. The page asks hunters to remove animal skins and parts, and it says no shooting is allowed outside hunting season. The geology note says the mountain is primarily Browns Park Formation chalky-white sandstone, capped by an ancient basalt flow, with small faults visible from the mountain. Vegetation listed is juniper, sagebrush, and mountain brush. Mammals listed are mule deer, marmot, fox, coyote, squirrels, and rabbits. Birds listed are golden eagles, redtail hawks, and turkey vultures. During nesting season the page says to stay off rock outcrops along the rim so fledglings are not disturbed. It also says cultural sites and artifacts are protected by federal law and should be left in place.

Directions on the BLM page: from Highway 40 on the west end of Craig, take Moffat County Road 7 north. The entrance road is on the right about half a mile past the Gun Club shooting range, and the day-use parking area is just up that road. The contact block lists 455 Emerson Street, Craig, Colorado, and the phone number (970) 826-5000. That is the office contact on the page, not the trailhead.

Questions

Is Cedar Mountain a national forest?

The Bureau of Land Management page describes it as an 880-acre Special Recreation Management Area on public land the BLM administers. It is day-use only, and the page lists the area as free.

How long is the Stearns Trail?

The BLM page says the Stearns Trail is 3.5 miles and starts at the parking area. A separate half-mile trail begins at the covered picnic tables.
