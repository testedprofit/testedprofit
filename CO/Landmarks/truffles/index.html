Truffles

What the survey states

Roadside Architecture's page of pig statues says the bronze sculpture of Truffles in Colorado Springs was created by Walt Horton in 2007 and was installed at this location by 2014. That is the whole entry. The page does not print a street address. Its map link opens a street-view image near latitude 38.8315254, longitude -104.8236849. This guide uses that camera position only as the location the survey's map link opens. It does not convert the camera into a street number, and it does not repeat 120 S Tejon Street or 102 S Tejon Street from the older page on this URL.

What is left out

A gallery page for a Horton pig sells a garden edition with its own measurements and an edition size. Those shop figures are not used as the measurements of the street sculpture, because the survey does not say the outdoor Truffles is that edition. A chocolate-shop walking loop that the older page attached to the pig is also left out. A separate roadside-inventory page that often lists animal statues did not load on this check, so no street address was taken from it. Until a public art record that loads gives a street address, the facts this guide will stand on are the sculptor, the 2007 date, the 2014 installation line, and the survey's map link.

Truffles is a bronze pig in the Colorado Springs entry of that survey. It is not the Pike Place Market pig Rachel, which the same page describes in the next entry, in Seattle, by a different sculptor. If you go looking for Truffles, use the survey map rather than a street address this guide does not have.

Questions

Who made Truffles, and when?

Roadside Architecture says Walt Horton created the bronze Truffles in 2007 and that it was installed at its Colorado Springs location by 2014. The entry does not print a street address.

Where is the map pin?

The survey's map link opens a street-view image near latitude 38.8315254, longitude -104.8236849. This guide does not turn that camera position into a street number.
