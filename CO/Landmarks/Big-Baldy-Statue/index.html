Big Baldy Statue

What the town lists

The Town of Dillon lists a facility named Big Baldy Statue at Highway 6 and Tenderfoot Street, Dillon, CO 80435. On the parks and recreation page saved by the Internet Archive on 13 June 2026, the amenities are access to the recreation path, access to the lakeshore, a pocket park, and public art. The listing's photograph is described as a statue of an eagle with wings outstretched. The live town page returned an access block on this check, so the archive copy is the record linked below.

Summit Daily, in a news story dated 3 October 2019, refers to the Big Baldy eagle sculpture overlooking Dillon Reservoir, between Lake Dillon Drive and Tenderfoot Street. That sentence places the eagle on U.S. Highway 6 in the same stretch the town's address uses. The story is about a highway crash, and this guide uses it only for the sculpture's name and place.

What was removed

The older page called Big Baldy a 2,800-pound bronze and copper eagle, and it added wheelchair parking, waste disposal, and a claim that the spot is one of the best sunrise and sunset viewpoints in Dillon. None of those details appears in the town listing or in the Summit Daily sentence. They are left out until a page that states them can be read. The town listing is enough to treat the site as public art in a pocket park with a path and lakeshore access.

Last updated 21 October 2026.

Questions

Where is Big Baldy?

The Town of Dillon facility list, as saved on 13 June 2026, gives Highway 6 and Tenderfoot Street, Dillon, CO 80435. Summit Daily on 3 October 2019 places the eagle sculpture between Lake Dillon Drive and Tenderfoot Street, overlooking Dillon Reservoir.

Is the weight or material posted here?

No. A 2,800-pound bronze-and-copper description was on the old page and is not in the town listing or the Summit Daily sentence checked here.
