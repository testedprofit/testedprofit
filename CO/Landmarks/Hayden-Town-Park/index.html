Hayden Town Park

The town pavilion

Hayden's facility-rental page lists a pavilion it calls 3rd Street / Town Park Pavilion. The posted cost is $50 for two hours, and the rental is subject to park availability. The same page lists a separate Dry Creek Park Pavilion at the same $50 rate for two hours. The two parks are different rentals.

Hayden Daze is at Dry Creek

The town calendar for 2026 Hayden Daze places the event at Dry Creek Park, 513 S Poplar Street, Hayden, CO 81639. The posted times are Friday, 17 July 2026, from 6:00 to 10:00 p.m., and Saturday, 18 July 2026, from 8:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. The listing says the event is free. It does not place Hayden Daze at Town Park. An older version of this page sent readers to Town Park for that festival. The calendar does not.

Courts listed at 229 S. 3rd Street

A Global Tennis Network court page, which invites users to submit corrections, lists Town Park at 229 S 3rd St, Hayden, Colorado 81639. That listing says the facility is public, with two hard courts, no indoor courts, no lights, and no court or member fees. Treat the street number, the court count, the surface, the lights, and the fees as that directory's listing, not as a line copied from the town's own rental page.

Questions

Where is Hayden Daze held?

The town calendar places 2026 Hayden Daze at Dry Creek Park, 513 S Poplar Street, Hayden, CO 81639, on Friday, 17 July 2026, from 6:00 to 10:00 p.m., and Saturday, 18 July 2026, from 8:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. It does not place the event at Town Park.

What does the town charge for the Town Park pavilion?

Hayden's facility-rental page lists the 3rd Street / Town Park Pavilion at $50 for two hours, subject to park availability. Dry Creek Park Pavilion is a separate rental at the same rate.
