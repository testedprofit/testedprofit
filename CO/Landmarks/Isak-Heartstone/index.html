Isak Heartstone

A Danish troll, still on Trollstigen

Isak Heartstone is a wooden troll by the Danish artist Thomas Dambo. Summit Daily's account of the 2019 move calls it a 15-foot-tall wooden sculpture and says Dambo is Danish. Breck Create's public-art page says the same: a large wooden troll by Thomas Dambo of Copenhagen, made from recycled materials, co-commissioned by the Town of Breckenridge and Breck Create. The page still places him on Trollstigen by the Illinois Creek trailhead and says the visit is an easy walk, bike ride, or free transit trip.

Why he moved

Summit Daily says the sculpture closed in fall 2018 after nearby residents complained about traffic and noise from visitors at the original site along the Wellington Trail. It says Dambo reimagined the troll, using the original head, heart, hands, and feet, and rebuilt it in May at a new site near the Stephen C. West Ice Arena and the Illinois Gulch trailhead. A photograph caption in that story places him along Illinois Creek on Monday, 24 June 2019. The story says he reopens to the public on Tuesday. The event line on the same page says June 25 at the Trollstigen trailhead, a 15-minute walk from the Breckenridge Welcome Center at 203 S. Main Street, and it encourages visitors to take the bus or walk rather than drive to the site. Breck Create lists an installation date of 21 June 2019. This guide keeps the 21 June installation date and the 25 June public reopening as the two dates those pages print.

The path in front of him

Summit Daily says the new trail, built by the town's open space and trails department with a local contractor, has a rock-lined path, 200 feet of wooden boardwalk, and a flagstone area in front of the troll for photos. The ribbon cutting in that account is at 2:30 p.m. at Trollstigen.

Last updated 21 October 2026.

Questions

Who made Isak Heartstone, and where is he now?

Summit Daily and Breck Create name the Danish artist Thomas Dambo. Breck Create places the wooden troll on Trollstigen by the Illinois Creek trailhead. Summit Daily says the rebuilt sculpture stands near the Stephen C. West Ice Arena and the Illinois Gulch trailhead.

When did the new site open?

Breck Create lists an installation date of 21 June 2019. Summit Daily's story of Monday, 24 June 2019, says the public reopening is Tuesday, and its event line says June 25 at Trollstigen.
