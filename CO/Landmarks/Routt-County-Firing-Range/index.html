Hayden Shooting Range

What the range page prints

Colorado Parks and Wildlife's shooting-range finder lists Hayden Shooting Range at the address it prints as 31253-31299 Routt City Rd, Hayden, CO 81639, with the contact 970-871-2855. The same page gives these directions: from Hayden, travel east on Highway 40 to Hawthorne Street / County Road 37, then continue south about 3 miles through the Y intersection, and the range is on the left. That directions block also uses the phrases Hayden County Shooting Ranges and Hayden Creek Shooting Range. The regulation heading for the same place is Hayden Shooting Range, Routt County. This guide quotes the printed address and the County Road 37 directions, and it does not treat Hayden County as a county name.

Hours, targets, and acreage

The state-wildlife-area page for Hayden Shooting Range says the site is 40 acres at elevation 6,431, in game management unit 13, and that no hunting opportunities are available. Its restrictions say public access is sunrise to sunset, that exploding targets and any targets other than paper and cardboard are prohibited, and that all rounds must impact designated berms except shotguns using birdshot. The March 2026 regulation text says public access is prohibited from sunset to sunrise, that exploding targets and any targets other than paper and cardboard are prohibited, and that all rounds must impact designated berms except shotguns using bird shot. Those are the rules used here.

The wildlife-area page also shows generic amenity labels for hunting, fishing, and camping. Those labels contradict the no-hunting line and the target rules on the same page, so this guide does not repeat them as activities at the range. The area office listed on that page is the Area 10 office in Steamboat Springs, 970-870-3333, which is not the range phone printed on the range finder. This page does not post a live open or closed status. A claim that a private club next door shares the site was not in these records and is left out.

Last updated 21 October 2026.

Questions

When is Hayden Shooting Range open, and what targets are allowed?

The wildlife-area page says sunrise to sunset. The March 2026 regulation says public access is prohibited from sunset to sunrise. Both say exploding targets and any targets other than paper and cardboard are prohibited, and that rounds must impact the designated berms except shotguns using bird shot.

How do the directions and the printed address differ?

The range finder prints 31253-31299 Routt City Rd, Hayden, CO 81639, and phone 970-871-2855. Its directions say east from Hayden on Highway 40 to Hawthorne Street / County Road 37, then south about 3 miles through the Y intersection, with the range on the left.
