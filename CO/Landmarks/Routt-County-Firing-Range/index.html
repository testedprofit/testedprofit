Hayden Shooting Range

Hayden Shooting Range is a Colorado Parks and Wildlife shooting range in Routt County, south of Hayden on County Road 37. It is 40 acres at elevation 6,431, in game management unit 13. Public access runs from sunrise to sunset. Paper and cardboard are the only targets. This is an independent Profitnet guide, not the CPW site.

The posted rules

The state wildlife area page and Chapter W-9, as approved in March 2026, use the same three limits for Hayden Shooting Range in Routt County.

  • Public access is sunrise to sunset. From sunset to sunrise, access is prohibited.
  • Exploding targets are prohibited. So is any target other than paper and cardboard.
  • Every round has to hit a designated berm. The exception is a shotgun using bird shot.

The wildlife-area page also says no hunting opportunities are available on these 40 acres. Bring paper or cardboard. Leave exploding targets, glass, metal, and anything else that is not paper or cardboard at home. The CPW range page titles the directions block Shooting at Hayden Creek Shooting Range and describes practice options there. The regulation heading for the hours and the berm rule is Hayden Shooting Range, Routt County. Use County Road 37 south of Hayden.

Those Hayden pages print the hours and the target rules. They do not print a day-use price. A Craig Daily Press roundup dated 14 August 2015 called this a free outdoor CPW range for rifle, pistol, and shotgun, open dawn to dusk. The current CPW wording is sunrise to sunset. Confirm anything about a fee with the range number before you treat the 2015 line as today's price.

Safety and range etiquette

Colorado Parks and Wildlife prints a shooting-safety block on its public range pages. The Park County range page carries it in full. While you are shooting, wear hearing protection and eye protection the whole time. Keep the muzzle pointed in a safe direction, one in which a bullet cannot strike a person, including ricochets. Treat every firearm as loaded, and check it yourself. Keep your finger off the trigger until you actually intend to shoot. Know your target and what is beyond it. At a range, that safe direction is downrange. Do not swing the muzzle out of that lane. Know where the other people on the line are.

CPW's same safety text gives the travel of a miss: a .22 short can travel more than a mile and a quarter, a high-velocity cartridge such as a .30-06 more than three miles, shotgun pellets about 500 yards, and a shotgun slug more than half a mile. That is why Hayden's rule puts every round, other than bird shot, into a designated berm. If you cannot see the berm, you are not ready to fire.

Etiquette at this range is the rule set above, practiced in daylight. Shoot only paper or cardboard. Point at the berm. Stop when the sun is down. The wildlife-area page lists an Area 10 office in Steamboat Springs at 970-870-3333, and the range finder lists 970-871-2855. Call one of those numbers if the road or the gate is not what the directions describe.

Other ranges in the 2015 roundup

The March 2026 Hayden section has no winter closure and no summer closure. Night is the closure. If Hayden is the wrong fit, the Craig Daily Press on 14 August 2015 listed other northwest Colorado ranges. Treat the hours in that story as 2015, and check the operator before you go.

  • Three Quarter Circles Sporting Clays, 26208 U.S. Highway 40, described then as open 1 May through 30 October, weather permitting, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. on weekends and weekdays by appointment.
  • Walden Public Shooting Area, a CPW range on Jackson County Road 12 off Colorado Highway 14, described then for pistol and rifle out to 200 yards, with no scattergun facilities.
  • Bears Ears Sportsman Club on Moffat County Road 7 near Craig, described then as a club range open to the public from 1 December through 31 March and on the first Friday and Saturday of each month.

Craig and Hayden sit in the same northwest corner of the state as two other guides on this site. Woodbury Park is the sports park in Craig. Bulldog Towing and Recovery is the independent Craig towing guide, at the number listings show for 21 S Ranney Street. Neither is a shooting range. The state list is the Colorado directory.

Questions

When is Hayden Shooting Range open, and what targets are allowed?

Public access is sunrise to sunset. The March 2026 regulation prohibits access from sunset to sunrise. Exploding targets are prohibited, and so is any target other than paper and cardboard. Rounds must hit the designated berms, except shotguns using bird shot.

How do I get there?

CPW prints the address as 31253-31299 Routt City Rd, Hayden, CO 81639, and the phone 970-871-2855. From Hayden, go east on Highway 40 to Hawthorne Street, County Road 37, then south about 3 miles through the Y intersection. The range is on the left. The map pin is 40.45834, -107.21855.
