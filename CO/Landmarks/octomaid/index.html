Iscariot

The title is Iscariot

The Downtown Partnership's Art on the Streets page titles the work Iscariot and credits Trace O'Connor of Fort Collins. It says the medium is salvaged steel: galvanized steel light poles, sprinkler piping, mechanical tubing, plate steel, and electrical conduit. It places the piece on the north side of Colorado Avenue between Interstate 25 and Sahwatch Street. The page describes a quasi-mermaid frozen just before flight. KOAA's 8 June 2018 story uses the headline nickname Octo-Maid and the official name Iscariot in the body. This guide uses Iscariot as the title and Octo-Maid only as that local nickname.

Steel only, or metal and wood

KOAA says the structure weighs 4,200 pounds and is comprised exclusively of reclaimed steel, mounted near the corner of Colorado Avenue and I-25 as part of Art on the Streets. The Gazette's 7 June 2018 story says a crane lifted the piece onto the building at 234 W Colorado Avenue. One caption dates that lift to Thursday, 7 June 2018, and calls the piece recycled metal and wood, made for the 20th anniversary of Art on the Streets. Another Gazette caption dates a lift to Thursday, 6 June 2018. This guide reports both captions. It also reports the material conflict: the Downtown Partnership and KOAA describe steel, and the Gazette caption adds wood.

The address 234 W Colorado Avenue, the north-side location between I-25 and Sahwatch, and the corner of Colorado Avenue and I-25 are the three location lines those sources print. They can describe one rooftop. This guide does not collapse them into a single phrasing the sources did not use, and it does not tell anyone to look up while driving. A roadside-inventory page about the sculpture did not load and is not linked.

Questions

What is the sculpture's name?

The Downtown Partnership and KOAA name it Iscariot, by Trace O'Connor of Fort Collins. KOAA's headline also calls it the Octo-Maid. The Downtown Partnership says it is salvaged steel on the north side of Colorado Avenue between I-25 and Sahwatch.

When was it installed, and what is it made of?

The Gazette dates a crane lift onto 234 W Colorado Avenue to Thursday, 7 June 2018, and another caption says Thursday, 6 June 2018. That caption says recycled metal and wood. KOAA says 4,200 pounds of reclaimed steel only. The Downtown Partnership lists steel materials and does not mention wood.
