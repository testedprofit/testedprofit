Octo-Maid

Octo-Maid on Colorado Avenue

Octo-Maid is the rooftop sculpture at 234 W Colorado Ave in Colorado Springs. KOAA's June 8, 2018 headline is Rise of the Octo-Maid. The story says the structure is massive, metal, and half octopus, weighs 4,200 pounds, and is comprised exclusively of reclaimed steel. It names that structure Iscariot and says it was mounted near the corner of Colorado Avenue and I-25 as part of the Downtown Partnership's Art on the Street program. Claire Swinford, urban engagement manager for the Downtown Partnership of Colorado Springs, is quoted in that story on the program.

The Gazette's June 7, 2018 photo story shows Fort Collins artist Trace O'Connor with his installation Iscariot. Captions call it a giant mermaid octopus lifted by crane to the top of the 234 W Colorado Ave. building. They call the piece recycled metal and wood, made for the 20th anniversary of the Art on the Streets project. One caption dates the lift to Thursday, June 7, 2018. Another caption dates a lift to Thursday, June 6, 2018. Both dates are printed on that Gazette page. The June 7 captions also say O'Connor spent about four hours welding the pieces together before the lift.

Iscariot on the Downtown Partnership page

The Downtown Partnership's Art on the Streets page for this work is titled Iscariot. It credits Trace O'Connor of Fort Collins, Colorado. It says the piece is recycled ferrous materials: galvanized steel light poles, sprinkler piping, mechanical tubing, plate steel, and electrical conduit. It describes quasi-mermaid lines, frozen just before flight. It places the work on the north side of Colorado Avenue between Interstate 25 and Sahwatch. That page does not mention wood. KOAA's 4,200 pounds of reclaimed steel, the Gazette's recycled metal and wood, and the Partnership's list of steel parts are the three material lines.

The Gazette's 234 W Colorado Ave. building, KOAA's corner of Colorado Avenue and I-25, and the Partnership's north side of Colorado Avenue between Interstate 25 and Sahwatch are the three location lines. KOAA's headline name is Octo-Maid. The Gazette captions and the Partnership page call the same installation Iscariot.

Questions

What is the sculpture at 234 W Colorado Ave called?

KOAA's June 8, 2018 headline calls it the Octo-Maid. The same story names the structure Iscariot and says it was mounted near the corner of Colorado Avenue and I-25. Gazette captions call the work lifted onto 234 W Colorado Ave Iscariot, a giant mermaid octopus by Trace O'Connor of Fort Collins. The Downtown Partnership page is titled Iscariot and places it on the north side of Colorado Avenue between Interstate 25 and Sahwatch.

When was it set in place, and what is it made of?

One Gazette caption dates a crane lift to Thursday, June 7, 2018, and calls the piece recycled metal and wood. Another Gazette caption dates a lift to Thursday, June 6, 2018. KOAA says 4,200 pounds of reclaimed steel only. The Downtown Partnership lists galvanized steel light poles, sprinkler piping, mechanical tubing, plate steel, and electrical conduit.
