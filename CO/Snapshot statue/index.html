Snapshot

In Addenbrooke Park

Snapshot is a multi-figure bronze in Addenbrooke Park in Lakewood. The National Sculptors' Guild dates the placement 15 June 1994 and says the Guild and Jane DeDecker placed it there. The Guild describes children on a bench with a wagon pulled up beside them, holding toys and drinking a soda, including the child in back who does not want to be in the picture. It says the piece was inspired by photographs Michael Jackson gave DeDecker. The Guild page quotes a Denver Post account that calls the park sculpture lifesize and says it depicts Jackson as a young boy with friends made younger for the piece: Macaulay Culkin, Gary Coleman, his sister Janet Jackson, his niece Brandi Jackson, and Brett Ratner, plus children who are friends of the sculptor.

Neverland, and the Lakewood casting

The Denver Post article dated 29 June 2009 says DeDecker met Jackson at an arts festival in Los Angeles in 1992, that he returned the next day and commissioned Snapshot, and that he sent photographs. It says she put a baseball glove on the young Jackson's left hand. It says Jackson had the original in his sculpture garden at Neverland, and that the one in Lakewood is a copy the city purchased in 1994 as its first piece of public art, set by a lake. The Guild's quotation of that reporting calls the Lakewood piece another casting from the edition, bought from the Guild. Kathy Berls, who the Post identifies as president of the Lakewood Arts Council when the work was chosen, told the paper the city had no idea of the Jackson connection until the reporter called. DeDecker told the Post she kept the commission low-key. The Guild quotation has her saying the piece is about multiculturalism and youth. This guide uses copy and another casting as the two words those pages use, and it uses lifesize only as the word in the Guild's quotation of the Post. The Post page calls it a bench sculpture.

Questions

Who made Snapshot, and when did Lakewood acquire it?

Jane DeDecker made it. The National Sculptors' Guild dates the Addenbrooke Park placement to 15 June 1994. The Denver Post says the city bought it in 1994 as its first piece of public art, and that Jackson had commissioned the original in 1992.

Who is in the group?

The Guild's quotation of the Denver Post names Jackson as a boy, Macaulay Culkin, Gary Coleman, Janet Jackson, Brandi Jackson, Brett Ratner, and friends of the sculptor. The Guild also describes a wagon, toys, a soda, and a child in back who does not want to be pictured.
