Furtaw Field

Oscoda Township's parks page describes Furtaw Field as a property in downtown Oscoda on US-23. It says the field covers 3.3 acres, functions as a town gathering spot, boasts a famous Paul Bunyan statue, and is used for community events. On the same page the township describes other public places, including Oscoda Beach Park on Lake Huron, which hosts the Art on the Beach arts and crafts show, and Riverbank Park on the Au Sable River.

Destination Oscoda calls Furtaw Field a community event field in downtown Oscoda, just off US-23. It gives the address as 415 North Lake Street, Oscoda, Michigan 48750. The listing says the field hosts Paul Bunyan Days and Oscoda Rockfest, a regional rock festival held there since 2019, with proceeds that support the Veteran Memorial Park Fund.

The Oscoda visitors page describes Paul Bunyan Days as an annual tradition of more than 45 years, held in mid-September at Furtaw Field, in the shadow of a 13-foot statue of the lumberjack. For 2026 it lists September 18, 19, and 20, with Friday hours from 4 p.m. to 10 p.m., Saturday from 9 a.m. to 10 p.m., and Sunday from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. The visitors page places a 13-foot statue at the field. The township calls the figure there a famous Paul Bunyan statue. The festival listing includes a chainsaw carving village, games, music, and food.

Last updated 21 October 2026.

Questions

What is Furtaw Field?

Oscoda Township says Furtaw Field, in downtown Oscoda on US-23, is a 3.3-acre property used as a town gathering spot. The township says it has a Paul Bunyan statue and is used for community events.

Which festivals use the field?

Destination Oscoda says the field, at 415 North Lake Street, hosts Paul Bunyan Days and Oscoda Rockfest, the rock festival held there since 2019. The Oscoda visitors page lists Paul Bunyan Days for September 18-20, 2026, beside a 13-foot statue.
