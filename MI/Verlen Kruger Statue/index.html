Verlen Kruger statue

The Verlen Kruger legacy site says the Verlen Kruger Memorial is on the banks of the Grand River at Thompson Field in Portland, Michigan. It says artist Derek Rainey designed the life-size statue of Verlen. It says the memorial was completed and dedicated in 2010, after work by many volunteers. The original committee named on the page is Mark Przedwojewski, Dan Smith, Stacy Smith Krause, Kevin Krause, Mike Smith, Kathy Lischkge, Joann Proctor, and Mike Leyrer. The page also credits Mike Doty and Kevin Schneider with later upkeep.

The Quiet Adventures Society publishes a program headed Verlen Kruger Memorial, Dedication June 26, 2010. The program places the dedication ceremony at Thompson Field and lists artist Derek Rainey speaking and pulling the cover off the statue. A morning memorial paddle was set to end at the same field. A later blessing of the rock, the program says, used water collected by paddlers from around the United States, poured on the rock the statue stands on.

The same legacy page distinguishes other markers from the Portland statue. In 2005 Kruger's name was placed on the Michigan Canoe Racing Association Canoer's Memorial Monument, along the River Road National Forest Scenic Byway, with a view of Cooke Pond on the Au Sable River in Iosco County near Oscoda. Kruger Landing, a separate accessible canoe launch, is on the Red Cedar River along the Lansing River Trail. The Portland figure is the life-size statue at Thompson Field.

The Grand River also has the Noahquageshik statue in Grand Rapids.

Questions

Where is the statue, and who designed it?

The Verlen Kruger legacy site says the memorial is on the banks of the Grand River at Thompson Field in Portland, Michigan, and that artist Derek Rainey designed the life-size statue.

When was it dedicated?

The legacy site says the dedication took place in 2010. The Quiet Adventures Society heads its program Verlen Kruger Memorial, Dedication June 26, 2010, and lists the unveiling by Derek Rainey at Thompson Field.
