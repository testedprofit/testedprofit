Schiller monument

The Detroit Historical Society holds a postcard titled Schiller Monument on Belle Isle, Detroit, Mich., dated about 1915. The catalog says the card shows a statue of the German poet and philosopher Friedrich Schiller. The monument is a seated bronze figure on a wide stone base. Carved text on the base says the monument was erected by citizens of German descent, Detroit, Michigan, 1907. The society lists the card in its postcard collection, 3.5 by 5.5 inches, under the search terms Belle Isle and monuments.

Wikipedia's article on Friedrich Schiller says there is a statue of him on Belle Isle in Detroit. It says this statue of the German playwright was commissioned by Detroit's German-American community in 1908, at a cost of $12,000, and that the designer was Herman Matzen. The postcard shows the seated bronze, the stone base, and the 1907 inscription. Wikipedia names Herman Matzen, the $12,000 cost, and a commission dated 1908.

A visitor on Belle Isle is looking for that seated bronze of the German poet, on the stone base whose inscription names the citizens of German descent and the year 1907. The Wikipedia sentence adds the designer's name and the cost recorded for the commission.
