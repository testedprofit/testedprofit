Stevie Wonder and Saginaw

Wikipedia says Stevie Wonder was born Stevland Hardaway Judkins on May 13, 1950, in Saginaw, Michigan, the third of five children of Lula Mae Hardaway and the second of her two children with Calvin Judkins. He was born six weeks premature. Wikipedia says that early birth, together with the oxygen-rich atmosphere of the hospital incubator, led to retinopathy of prematurity, which left him blind. The findings of H.R. 1566, a bill introduced in the House on March 19, 2007, to award him a Congressional Gold Medal, state that Stevland Hardaway Judkins, later known as Stevie Wonder, was born in Saginaw, Michigan, to Lula Mae Hardaway on May 13, 1950, and that he was born prematurely and placed in an incubator where an excess of oxygen worsened retinopathy of prematurity and caused his blindness.

Wikipedia says that when he was four, his mother divorced his father and moved with her three children to Detroit. He sang in the choir at Whitestone Baptist Church and became a soloist at age eight. He played piano, harmonica, and drums early, and in 1961 Motown signed him and his surname was legally changed to Morris, which his mother's authorized biography calls an old family name. The professional name was Little Stevie Wonder. He signed with Motown's Tamla label at 11. Wikipedia says his single Fingertips topped the Billboard Hot 100 when he was 13. After the 1962 album The Jazz Soul of Little Stevie, Wikipedia says, he enrolled in the Michigan School for the Blind in Lansing. The 2007 bill's findings say that the year after the 1963 hit he enrolled in the Michigan School for the Blind and studied classical piano.

Wikipedia says he has won 25 Grammy Awards, the most by a male solo artist, and that he has been inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame, the Rhythm and Blues Music Hall of Fame, and the Songwriters Hall of Fame. Dorsey College's page of facts about Saginaw says Steveland Hardaway Judkins was born in Saginaw in 1950 and that he has been honored with a plaque at the Juneteenth Creative Cultural Center in Saginaw. Wikipedia and the bill spell the birth name Stevland. The college page spells it Steveland.
