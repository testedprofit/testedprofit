Saint Joseph Church and Shrine

Wikipedia places the Saint Joseph Church and Shrine at 8742 U.S. Route 12 in the Irish Hills, in rural Cambridge Township, Lenawee County, Michigan. It says the district was designated a Michigan Historic Site on April 19, 1990, and was added to the National Register of Historic Places on May 4, 2007, reference number 07000382. The infobox lists the architectural styles as Spanish Colonial Revival and Mission Revival, and names Dionicio Rodriguez, Leo Ouelette, and Ralph Corona.

The article traces the church to a missionary founding in 1854 by the area's earliest Irish settlers. Irish Catholics had come into the area from the 1830s and met in private homes for occasional Mass. By 1852 there were enough families that a church was needed, and Humphrey and Ann Agan, and Martin Kelly, deeded the site to the Bishop of Detroit. Work began in 1854 and continued on and off until 1862. Wikipedia says the original church was a simple fieldstone building. The tower, sacristy, and stained glass were added in 1911. A new roof, transept, and sanctuary were added in 1928. In 1932 the shrine was completed under the supervision of Leo Ouelette, Dionicio Rodriguez, and Ralph Corona. The first resident priest arrived in 1954, and the article says the church continues as a Catholic church.

The historic district includes the church, the shrine, and a cemetery with graves dating to the 1850s. Wikipedia describes the church as a fieldstone cruciform building with a hip roof, a square bell tower at the front, and red ceramic tile on the church and the tower. A 1954 addition sits in one corner.

The Village of Onsted's history places the village in Michigan's Irish Hills and says the village itself was mapped in February 1883, when William Onsted, J. Edwards Gibbs, and Charles Greenleaf laid it out beside the railroad. On the churches of the early settlers, the village history says Irish Catholic settlers organized St. Joseph Church in 1854, built largely from locally gathered fieldstone, with construction that took several years. It calls Onsted a gateway to the Irish Hills. The Chicago Road, now U.S. Highway 12, is the route the village history follows through that region. Wikipedia gives the church's address on that highway, in rural Cambridge Township.
