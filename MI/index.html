Michigan landmark guides

This page is the index of Michigan landmark guides on TestedProfit. Eighteen guides are filed under the Michigan directory, and each card uses the title of the page it opens. The Commodore Perry statue in that list stands in Perrysburg, Ohio, and Sculpture in the Park stands in Ottawa Hills, Ohio. Earning-app guides and the national park archive are linked from the related list, separate from these cards. The Louisiana directory is linked there as the previous state section.

The eighteen cards use the titles of the guides they open. The list begins with the brown trout sculpture in Baldwin and runs through the other Michigan guides filed at these addresses. A card and the page it opens use the same title.
