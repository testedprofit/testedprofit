Skanderbeg statue

The equestrian statue catalog lists a statue of Skanderbeg, George Kastrioti, in the United States, with the town line MI Rochester, the year 2006, and the sculptor Kreshnik Xhiku, described there as an Albanian sculptor working in the United States. The rider line gives the years 1405-1468. The catalog's account says Sultan Murad II took him hostage in 1423 and that he fought for the Ottoman Empire for the following twenty years. In 1443 he deserted and became the ruler of Krujë. His first victory against the Ottomans in 1444 began more than twenty years of war. The catalog says his forces won more than twenty victories in the field and withstood three sieges of Krujë, and that Albania regards him as its national hero.

Spanish Wikipedia's article on Kreshnik Xhiku says the Skanderbeg monument in Michigan was inaugurated on September 24, 2006, before several thousand people, among them Detroit Archbishop Cardinal Adam Maida and the president of Albania, Alfred Moisiu. It says the sculpture was installed beside a road at the entrance to the courtyard of St. Paul's Albanian Catholic Church, about 40 kilometers north of Detroit, and it spells the place Rocester Hills. It names Dom Antón Kqira as one of the initiators. The sculpture, the article says, was the fruit of a year of work by the Albanian-American sculptor, who had won the competition to make the monument in May 2005. The article gives the coordinates 42°38′5″N, 83°8′30″W.

Fjala, in an article on the monument's twentieth year, calls it the first statue of Skanderbeg in the United States, inaugurated in 2006 in Rochester Hills. It places the equestrian bronze at 525 W Auburn Rd, beside the Albanian Catholic Church of St. Paul, and names the sculptor Kreshnik Xhiku. The catalog's town line reads Rochester. Fjala writes Rochester Hills and gives the street address. Spanish Wikipedia writes Rocester Hills and places the figure at the courtyard entrance. All three date the statue to 2006 and name Xhiku.

Another monument in this directory is the Kosciuszko monument in Detroit.

Questions

Who made the statue, and when was it inaugurated?

The equestrian statue catalog names sculptor Kreshnik Xhiku and the year 2006. Spanish Wikipedia says the monument was inaugurated on September 24, 2006, with Detroit Archbishop Cardinal Adam Maida and Albanian President Alfred Moisiu present.

Where does it stand?

Fjala places the equestrian bronze at 525 W Auburn Rd in Rochester Hills, beside the Albanian Catholic Church of St. Paul. Spanish Wikipedia places it at the entrance to the church courtyard and spells the city Rocester Hills.
