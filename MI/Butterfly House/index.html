Butterfly House

The Greater Port Austin Area Chamber of Commerce welcomes visitors to the Butterfly House at Water Tower Park in downtown Port Austin. The chamber calls it a tranquil place of natural beauty and community engagement, with colorful wings and blooms. Butterfly releases begin in late June and continue through the summer months, with a large release held the Friday before the Fourth of July. Inside, benches sit around the sound of a bubbler. Outside, a picnic area is open. The chamber also offers one-hour private releases for a birthday, an anniversary, or a commemoration, and it mentions art displays from local art organizations.

The location line gives 8699 Adams Street and the phone number (989) 738-7600, with the email chamber@portaustinarea.com. The release notes say every release depends on the weather. The first release takes place in late June, and the last takes place from early to mid-September. Releases happen at varying times through the summer. Butterflies are released into the wild in early October.

The same page invites volunteers to help the Butterfly House and Community Garden with garden maintenance, releases, and events. A visit is a stop at Water Tower Park on Adams Street, timed to the chamber's summer release season, with the picnic area and the indoor benches around the bubbler.
