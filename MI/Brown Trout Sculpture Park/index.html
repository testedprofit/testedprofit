Brown trout sculpture

Pleasant Plains Township describes the Baldwin Brown Trout Sculpture Park as the home of the world's largest brown trout sculpture. The township says the piece was dedicated in 2018 and was commissioned by the Village of Baldwin, thanks to the donations of many people. Donor names are set along the walkway of the sculpture park in downtown Baldwin.

The Lake County Chamber of Commerce places Baldwin, the county seat, at the junction of M-37 and US-10, near the Baldwin River, a tributary of the Pere Marquette River. The chamber says the Baldwin River is the site of the first planting of German brown trout roe in the United States, on April 11, 1884. It lists a trailhead to the Pere Marquette Rail to Trail at Brown Trout Sculpture Park among the village's public places, beside the chamber center at 895 Michigan Avenue and the historical museum.

Michigan Trails describes the 58.5-mile Pere Marquette State Trail on a former CSX railroad line from Baldwin to Clare. It says the 33-mile section from Baldwin to Evart begins at the trailhead in downtown Baldwin, where a visitor sees the World's Largest Brown Trout Sculpture, measuring 25 feet from front tooth to tail fin. Together they place a 2018 sculpture in downtown Baldwin, tie the Baldwin River to the April 11, 1884 planting, and give the fish a length of 25 feet from front tooth to tail fin.

Questions

Where is the brown trout sculpture, and when was it dedicated?

Pleasant Plains Township says the Baldwin Brown Trout Sculpture Park in downtown Baldwin holds the world's largest brown trout sculpture, commissioned by the Village of Baldwin and dedicated in 2018.

How tall is it, and what does the trail guide say?

Michigan Trails says the World's Largest Brown Trout Sculpture measures 25 feet from front tooth to tail fin at the downtown Baldwin trailhead of the Pere Marquette State Trail.
