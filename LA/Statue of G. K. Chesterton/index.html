G. K. Chesterton statue

The Autumn 2016 issue of The Defendant, the newsletter of the Australian Chesterton Society, says a small town called Ponchatoula, in Louisiana, boasts the only life-size statue of Chesterton in the world. It says the statue stands prominently in front of an events hall called Chesterton Square. The newsletter calls the statue the brainchild of a local doctor, Robert Benson, who was inspired to honor Chesterton after reading his writings on marriage and family in the anthology Brave New Family, published by Ignatius Press in 1990.

The newsletter is writing about a statue it says already stands in Ponchatoula, in front of the events hall called Chesterton Square, and it is the newsletter that calls the figure the only life-size statue of Chesterton in the world. It credits the project to Robert Benson.

In 2008, History News Network pointed to a Religion in American History post and said the first statue of G. K. Chesterton in the United States would be unveiled later that year in the town, which the network spelled Paschatoula. It described the town as an hour north of New Orleans on the shores of Lake Pontchartrain. History News Network was describing a statue expected later in 2008. The 2016 newsletter places a life-size statue in front of Chesterton Square in Ponchatoula and names Robert Benson.
