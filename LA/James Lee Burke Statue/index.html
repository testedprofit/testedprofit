James Lee Burke statue

The Daily Iberian reported on April 8, 2024, that a statue of the writer James Lee Burke was unveiled the previous Saturday in Bouligny Plaza in downtown New Iberia, during the Books Along the Teche Literary Festival. The paper said the sculpture shows Burke in his signature cowboy hat. Houston-based sculptor Shirley Scarpetta made a life-size depiction of the author. Local residents led by Paul Schexnayder organized the project after the funding was in place. Scarpetta was at the unveiling and spoke at the Sliman Theater with Schexnayder. Burke joined an hour-long question-and-answer session by Zoom that day and said he was humbled. Mayor Freddie DeCourt spoke before the unveiling and called the statue another connection to the city's history and culture.

The April 8 story says Burke has lived in New Iberia in the past and centers the plots of his Dave Robicheaux novels in the city. It says the popularity of the books has drawn visitors who want to see the city he writes about. A follow-up in the same paper on April 15, 2024, is headed James Lee Burke in Bronze. It says the sculpture of the author was unveiled in Bouligny Plaza on Saturday, April 6, 2024, during the festival, and that sculptor Shirley Scarpetta was among those present. That caption calls him a New Iberia native. The April 8 story is the account of his having lived in the city and of the novels set there.

NOLA.com, writing about Burke's novel Clete, says a statue of the author by Houston sculptor Shirley Scarpetta was unveiled earlier that year during the Books Along the Teche Literary Festival. In that article Burke says he is honored, calls it a bronze statue and a big one, and hopes the robins that come through New Iberia each year will take care of it. The Daily Iberian's life-size account, the April 15 bronze heading, and Burke's remark in NOLA.com are the size and material details these reports give. The place they name for the unveiling is Bouligny Plaza.

Questions

When and where was the James Lee Burke statue unveiled?

The Daily Iberian says the statue was unveiled on Saturday, April 6, 2024, in Bouligny Plaza in New Iberia, during the Books Along the Teche Literary Festival.

Who made it, and what does it show?

The Daily Iberian credits Houston-based sculptor Shirley Scarpetta with a life-size depiction of Burke in his cowboy hat. Burke told NOLA.com it is a bronze statue.
