Huey Long statue and grave

The statue of Huey P. Long is the center of the Louisiana State Capitol grounds in Baton Rouge. He is buried beneath it. Chas. Keck made the figure, and it was erected in 1940.

Plan your visit

Place
Center of the State Capitol garden, Baton Rouge. The grounds cover 27 acres.
What to look for
A standing figure on a tall stone pedestal, with the capitol tower behind it. Long is buried under the statue.
The walks
Two main walkways, each about twenty feet wide, cross the grounds. Oaks, magnolias, and azaleas fill the lawns.
The building
The capitol Long built rises 450 feet and is still the tallest state capitol in the United States.

The figure at the center of the garden

The Capitol grounds are 27 acres of native plants, kept close, with oaks, magnolias, and azaleas. Two walkways, each twenty feet across, organize the lawn. A narrower path circles the outside, and smaller paths tie that circle to the main walks. Boxwood hedges border the paths. Camellias and azaleas line the inside of the main walks, and evergreens, juniper alternating with arborvitae, line the outside. The focal point of all of that is the statue of Governor and U.S. Senator Huey P. Long.

Because the capitol was his project, he is buried under the statue. The figure was erected in 1940 to mark the spot. A photograph record of the State Capitol garden names the sculptor as Chas. Keck and places the statue and grave at the center of the garden, erected in 1940. The photograph on this page is that view from the garden: the bronze seen from behind, the pedestal, and the tower rising over the hedges.

Who he was

Huey Pierce Long Jr. was born on 30 August 1893 near Winnfield, in Winn Parish. He served as Louisiana's governor from 1928 to 1932 and as a United States senator from 1932 until he was killed in 1935. He was a left-wing populist Democrat who became a national figure in the Depression by arguing that Franklin Roosevelt's New Deal fell short. In office he expanded social programs and built roads, bridges, hospitals, and schools on a scale the South had not seen. The new capitol, at 450 feet, is still the tallest capitol in the country. He is celebrated as a champion of the poor and denounced as a demagogue. Both reputations travel with the statue.

The night in September 1935

On Sunday, 8 September 1935, Long was at the capitol to pass a bill that would redraw the district of an opponent, Judge Benjamin Pavy. At 9:20 p.m., just after the bill passed, Pavy's son-in-law, Carl Weiss, approached Long and, in the account generally accepted, fired one shot from a few feet away. Long's bodyguards fired back and killed Weiss. Long died of the wound. More than 200,000 people came to Baton Rouge for the funeral on 12 September. His remains were buried on the capitol grounds, and a statue was built on the grave. The House date for that statue is 1940, five years after the shooting.

How to see it

Come onto the capitol grounds and follow the wide walks to the center of the garden. The tower is the backdrop. The statue is the grave marker, not a figure inside the building. Read the pedestal, walk once around the hedges, and you have the stop. The Hebe statue elsewhere in Baton Rouge, and the Lafayette bronze on University Avenue in Lafayette, are other Louisiana figures. This one belongs to the capitol lawn.

Use the tower as the landmark from the street. It is 450 feet, still the tallest state capitol in the country, and the garden sits at its foot. The two twenty-foot walks meet the statue, so you are not hunting through side paths. Oaks and magnolias shade the lawn, and the azaleas and camellias mark the edges of the main walks. The photograph is the view from the garden, looking past the bronze toward the entrance steps.

Questions

Where is the Huey Long statue?

At the center of the Louisiana State Capitol garden in Baton Rouge. Long is buried beneath it.

Who made it, and when?

Chas. Keck. It was erected in 1940.

When was Long killed?

Carl Weiss shot him inside the capitol on 8 September 1935. The funeral was on 12 September, and he was buried on the grounds.
