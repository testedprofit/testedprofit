Benito Juárez statue

Wikipedia's article on the Statue of Benito Juárez in New Orleans names the artist Juan Fernando Olaguíbel and the subject Benito Juárez, president of Mexico from 1858 to 1872. It places the statue at 1200 Conti Street, New Orleans, LA 70112. The article says Juárez lived in New Orleans for two separate periods in the mid-nineteenth century, each time because the Oaxaca-born lawyer was exiled from Mexico for political reasons.

Wikipedia says the statue was part of a project launched by Mayor Chep Morrison in 1957, in which a median on Basin Street would become a monument to New Orleans' place in the history of the Western Hemisphere, called the Garden of the Americas. The garden was also to include monuments to Simón Bolívar and Francisco Morazán. In 1965, the article says, the Mexican government gave the city the statue of Juárez, which was placed in the neighborhood where he had lived in exile. On May 17, 1972, one hundred years after Juárez's death, the statue was dedicated by the XII Mexico-United States Inter-parliamentary Conference.

MyNewOrleans, writing in 2010 about the Bolívar statue, says a statue of Simón Bolívar was erected in 1957 on the Basin Street neutral ground at Canal Street, and that in 1965 and 1966 statues of Benito Juárez and Francisco Morazán were added, forming the Garden of the Americas. The magazine says the garden was created to acknowledge New Orleans' connection to the Hispanic world and the heroes of Venezuela, Mexico, and Honduras. A Tulane Stone Center note on a WGNO segment says the three statues stand on the neutral ground of Basin Street, that Mayor Chep Morrison commissioned the sculptures installed between 1957 and 1966, and that the Juárez monument was a gift from the Mexican government. Wikipedia names the sculptor, the Conti Street address, the 1965 gift, and the May 17, 1972 dedication. MyNewOrleans and the Tulane note describe the three statues together on the Basin Street neutral ground.

Questions

Who sculpted the New Orleans Juárez statue, and where does it stand?

Wikipedia names Juan Fernando Olaguíbel and places the statue at 1200 Conti Street in New Orleans.

When did the statue arrive, and when was it dedicated?

Wikipedia says the Mexican government gave the statue to the city in 1965 and that it was dedicated on May 17, 1972, the hundredth anniversary of Juárez's death.
