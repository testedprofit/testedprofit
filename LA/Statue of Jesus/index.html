Sacred Heart of Jesus statue

MyNewOrleans published an article under the headline Touchdown Jesus Celebrates 200 Years about the Sacred Heart of Jesus statue at St. Louis Cathedral. The magazine says the statue was carved by the Italian sculptor S. Lenzi of Pietrasanta and is made from Carrara marble from the Apuan Alps. It was unveiled in the cathedral garden on May 7, 1926, by the Merilhs' sole grandchild, Miss Louis Georgette Tusson. The Very Rev. Father Albert Antoine of St. Louis Cathedral gave the blessing. The magazine says the statue was damaged in Hurricane Katrina in 2005, when several fingers were knocked off as trees fell around the gardens, and that the hand was repaired in 2015. It says Archbishop Gregory Aymond gave the blessing and treated the repair as a symbol of hope while the city was still recovering.

FOX8, in a story published August 24, 2015, says the newly renovated Sacred Heart statue of Jesus stands behind St. Louis Cathedral and that Archbishop Gregory Aymond unveiled it on Sunday morning. The station says the statue lost several fingers and sustained damage during Hurricane Katrina, and that Aymond had promised it would not be repaired until the city was rebuilt. With the tenth anniversary approaching, the story quotes him saying some people were still rebuilding and that the restored hand was also a sign of hope.

NBC News, reporting the cathedral's first Sunday Mass after Katrina, says a marble statue of Jesus Christ outside the church, with his arm outstretched, had the thumb and forefinger snapped off in the storm. It quotes Archbishop Alfred Hughes asking that those fingers not be reattached until the recovery of New Orleans was further along. NBC's 2005 report names Archbishop Alfred Hughes and the missing thumb and forefinger. FOX8 in 2015 and MyNewOrleans describe the later repair, several fingers, and Archbishop Gregory Aymond. MyNewOrleans is also where the Lenzi carving, the Carrara marble, and the May 7, 1926 unveiling in the cathedral garden are described.

The Joan of Arc statue, farther down Decatur Street, has its own guide: Joan of Arc on Decatur Street.

Questions

Where is the Sacred Heart statue, and who carved it?

MyNewOrleans says the Carrara marble statue was carved by S. Lenzi of Pietrasanta, Italy, and unveiled in the cathedral garden on May 7, 1926. FOX8 says the statue stands behind St. Louis Cathedral.

What happened to the hand in Hurricane Katrina?

MyNewOrleans says several fingers were knocked off in 2005 and the hand was repaired in 2015. NBC News, in 2005, said the thumb and forefinger had snapped off and quoted Archbishop Alfred Hughes asking that they stay off until the city's recovery was further along.
