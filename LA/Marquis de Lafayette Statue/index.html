Marquis de Lafayette statue

The National War Memorial Registry lists a memorial to Gilbert du Motier, Marquis de Lafayette, at 705 West University Avenue in Lafayette, Louisiana, zip code 70506. The listing gives the year dedicated as 1987, marks access as public, and classifies the memorial as a statue of a high-ranking military officer from the Revolutionary War. The tribute line on the listing reads as a tribute from the people of Lafayette, Louisiana, to the Marquis de La Fayette.

A page of Lafayette statues in the United States on Rapiere includes a caption for a statue of General Lafayette by Charles Correia, dated 1987, in Lafayette, Louisiana, with a photograph credit to Lafayette Travel. The next lines call it an equestrian statue of La Fayette at the Parc Lafayette complex in Lafayette, and the English line says the statue is at the rear of that complex.

The registry gives the street address, the 1987 dedication year, and public access. The Rapiere caption names Charles Correia, the same year, and an equestrian statue at the rear of the Parc Lafayette complex. Both describe the Lafayette figure on West University Avenue.
