Marquis de Lafayette statue

The National War Memorial Registry lists a memorial to Gilbert du Motier, Marquis de Lafayette, at 705 West University Avenue in Lafayette, Louisiana, zip code 70506, dedicated in 1987, with public access. A Rapiere caption names a 1987 statue of General Lafayette by Charles Correia at the Parc Lafayette complex.

Two descriptions of the same 1987 figure

The registry's location line is 705 West University Avenue, Lafayette, Louisiana, zip code 70506. The year dedicated is 1987. Access is public. The type is a statue of a high-ranking military officer, and the war is the Revolutionary War. The tribute line reads as a tribute from the people of Lafayette, Louisiana, to the Marquis de La Fayette.

On Rapiere's page of Lafayette statues in the United States, a caption reads: statue of General Lafayette by Charles Correia (1987) in Lafayette, Louisiana, with the photograph credited to Lafayette Travel. The next line, in French, calls it an equestrian statue of La Fayette in front of the Parc Lafayette complex, and the English line says the statue is at the rear of that complex. The registry supplies the street address and the public-access note. Rapiere supplies the sculptor, the equestrian form, and the complex name. Both use 1987 and Lafayette, Louisiana.

What the pages do not settle

The registry prints the street address, the 1987 year, public access, and the Revolutionary War classification. The Rapiere caption prints Charles Correia, the same year, an equestrian statue, the Parc Lafayette complex, and a photograph credit to Lafayette Travel. The French line calls it an equestrian statue in front of the Parc Lafayette complex. The English line says the statue is at the rear of that complex. Neither page gives a height, a foundry, or a dedication-day program. The map pin is the street address from the registry, 30.2204741, -92.0246667, not a separate monument node.

A visitor using both pages can match the year and the city first, then use the registry for the door number and Rapiere for the sculptor and the rear-of-complex note. The tribute line on the registry is the people of Lafayette, Louisiana, to the Marquis de La Fayette. That line is the registry's wording for who the memorial honors. It does not name a dedication speaker.

The Rapiere page is a list of Lafayette statues in the United States, and the 1987 Correia caption is one entry on it. Nearby captions on that page include a 1976 bronze in LaGrange, Georgia, credited to Visit LaGrange, a 1937 concrete and plaster statue by Arnold Foerster in Westlake's Lafayette Park in Los Angeles, and a statue in front of the Golden Plough Tavern in York, Pennsylvania. Those are other cities. They show how the page labels each figure with a year and a place. For Lafayette, Louisiana, the label is Charles Correia, 1987, Parc Lafayette complex, photograph by Lafayette Travel.

The registry classifies the West University Avenue memorial as a Revolutionary War statue of a high-ranking military officer and marks access public. Use that classification for the kind of memorial the registry thinks it is. Use the Rapiere caption for the equestrian form and the rear of the complex. The pin at 30.2204741, -92.0246667 is the street address, so the last turn is the complex the caption describes, not a different Lafayette monument in the French Quarter.

Other Louisiana guides on this site include the James Lee Burke statue in New Iberia and the Huey Long statue at the state capitol in Baton Rouge. They are different works.

Questions

Where is the Lafayette memorial, and when was it dedicated?

The National War Memorial Registry lists it at 705 West University Avenue in Lafayette, dedicated in 1987, with public access.

Who is credited with the statue?

A Rapiere caption identifies a 1987 statue of General Lafayette by Charles Correia in Lafayette, Louisiana, and calls the figure an equestrian statue at the Parc Lafayette complex.

Do both sources print the street address?

The registry prints 705 West University Avenue. The Rapiere caption prints the sculptor, the year, and the Parc Lafayette complex, and it does not print that street number.
