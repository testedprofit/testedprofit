Geese fountain

Kent Ullberg's list of selected commissions includes a monument called Geese Fountain, commissioned by the City of Lake Charles, Louisiana, for the Bord dee Lac/Lakefront Promenade and Marina. The list dates the installation to September 2010. The sculptor's page uses the spelling Bord dee Lac in that line.

KPLC reported, in a story updated August 22, 2010, that the crowning jewel of the Lakefront Promenade was set to be installed. The station said the statue by wildlife sculptor Kent Ullberg features three greater white-fronted geese, set in bronze, double sized, and created to look as though they are taking flight. Ullberg told the station the work took more than a year and a half. He said installation would begin Monday morning at 8 a.m. and that he would supervise the plumbing and the placement through the next week, including the goose on top, which he wanted to look as if it were barely touching.

A later KPLC report on the promenade's grand opening describes a ribbon cutting on the lakefront. It says the project includes a walking path along the water, the Louisiana Landing Fountain, and a new Bord du Lac Marina. Mayor Randy Roach is quoted on the space the promenade gives the city for events. The geese story and the opening story are both about that lakefront project. Ullberg's commission list is the record that names Geese Fountain, the city commission, and the September 2010 installation. KPLC is the record for the three geese, the bronze, and the taking-flight pose.

Questions

Who made the Lake Charles geese, and when were they installed?

Kent Ullberg's commission list names Geese Fountain for the City of Lake Charles and dates the installation to September 2010. KPLC described three greater white-fronted geese in bronze.

Where does the sculptor's list place the fountain?

Ullberg's list places Geese Fountain at the Bord dee Lac/Lakefront Promenade and Marina, using that spelling. KPLC's promenade report names a Bord du Lac Marina.
