Mother River statue

The Port of New Orleans describes the plaza at the Port Administration Building in a rental sheet. The sheet calls the plaza an outdoor, brick-covered space in front of the building, with panoramic views of the Mississippi, and it names the Mother River statue as the focal point.

The same sheet gives the plaza's dimensions as 37,720 square feet. It lists a maximum capacity of 500 for a reception, and it says seated functions will vary. Availability is printed as 8 a.m. to 10 p.m., seven days a week. The fee includes use of the space on the river side of the Port Administration Building, between the visitors parking lot and the employee parking lot, and the sheet says that fee does not include indoor facilities.

A visitor looking for the Mother River statue is looking for that outdoor brick plaza on the river side of the administration building, between the visitors parking lot and the employee parking lot. The sheet lists the plaza for receptions of up to 500 people, from 8 a.m. to 10 p.m., seven days a week, and it names the statue as the focal point in front of the building, with the Mississippi in view.
