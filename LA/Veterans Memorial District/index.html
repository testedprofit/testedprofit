South Lafourche veterans memorial

The LA Cajun Bayou tourism listing describes the South Lafourche Veteran's Memorial as a landscaped memorial with a 122-foot granite wall monument and over 3,000 names of veterans of South Lafourche Parish. The listing gives the address as 16937 Highway 3235, Cut Off, LA 70345, and the phone number 985-475-7702.

A 2022 financial report filed with the Louisiana Legislative Auditor covers the Veterans' Memorial District of Ward 10 of Lafourche Parish. The notes say the Legislature created the district in its 2005 regular session by R.S. 33:9356. They say the governing authority is a board of nine commissioners who are electors domiciled in Ward 10, and that a minimum of six members are drawn from veterans' posts in the ward. The report's contact line for the treasurer, Larry Raymond, uses the same street address, 16937 Highway 3235, Cut Off, LA 70345.

The tourism listing gives a visitor the wall's 122-foot length, its granite, a count of over 3,000 names, and the phone number 985-475-7702. The audit identifies the district that maintains the memorial, the 2005 act that created it, and the Cut Off address used for treasurer Larry Raymond. The wall and the district share 16937 Highway 3235.
