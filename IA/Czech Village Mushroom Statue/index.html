Czech Village morel

98.1 KHAK, in a post dated 11 May 2021, says Houby Days was returning to Czech Village that Friday, Saturday, and Sunday after a disappointing 2020. The station says houby is Czech for mushrooms, and that 2021 was the 43rd anniversary of the festival. It says visitors could see a new 7-foot-tall morel mushroom carved by chainsaw artist Nate Greiner out of a tree that fell during the previous year’s derecho. The derecho year in that sentence is 2020.

KHAK says 16th Avenue would be blocked for street vendors and activities for children, and it attributes that line to a Homegrown Iowan article. The station also says a CBS 2 article reported that the queen contest, the carnival, and the parade would not happen in 2021. Local businesses would offer specials, and the Czech Plus Band would play from 7 p.m. to 8:30 p.m. on the Friday.

The pages do not call the carving a stump behind a numbered address, and they do not list viewing hours. The place they do name is Czech Village, on the 16th Avenue festival street, with a 7-foot morel by Nate Greiner made from a derecho tree.

A later festival story was not needed to date the carving. The May 2021 station post is the account.

Last updated 21 October 2026.

Questions

Who carved the morel, and how tall is it?

KHAK’s 11 May 2021 post says chainsaw artist Nate Greiner carved a 7-foot-tall morel from a tree that fell in the previous year’s derecho. It places the carving in Czech Village for Houby Days.

What does houby mean?

KHAK says houby is Czech for mushrooms. The same post calls 2021 the 43rd anniversary of Houby Days and says 16th Avenue would be blocked for the festival.
