Maasdam wagon wheels

Grow Jasper County, the county’s business listing, places the L.J. Maasdam wagon-wheel sculpture at S. 48th Avenue E, Jasper County, Grinnell, Iowa 50112. It calls the work a roadside stop a short trip off Interstate 80. The listing says the art includes more than 200 large wagon wheels welded together, and that L.J. Maasdam constructed it in 1994 when he was 90. A picnic table is on the site.

That listing does not give a height, a death date, or turn-by-turn directions from an interstate exit. The address it does print is S. 48th Avenue E. The wheels are the sculpture. The listing does not describe a cornfield backdrop or a lighting schedule.

The page is a county directory entry, not a newspaper profile. It is the account for the maker, the year, the age, the wheel count, and the road. Visitors using that address are on a rural road south of Grinnell, at the sculpture the listing describes.

No second public page loaded with a different wheel count or a different year. The figures to use are more than 200 wheels, 1994, and age 90.

Questions

Where are the wagon wheels?

Who built them, and when?

The listing says L.J. Maasdam welded more than 200 large wagon wheels in 1994, when he was 90.
