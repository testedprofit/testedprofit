Lincoln Statue

The Lincoln statue stands at the junction of Lincoln Way and Chestnut Street in Jefferson, on the south side of the public square in front of the Greene County Courthouse. The National Register nomination calls it a life-size bronze. Wikipedia also calls the bronze life size. It is not described in those pages as an 8-foot figure. The nomination says the left arm hangs down holding a piece of paper and the right arm is extended slightly. It does not describe a hand raised to the chin.

The nomination says Capitol Hill Monument Co. of Des Moines put up a temporary concrete base for the dedication on 22 September 1918. That base was replaced in 1964, and both bronze plaques were remounted. The lower base measures 6 feet 6 inches by 6 feet 6 inches by 44 inches, with a pebblestone finish. The upper base measures 4 feet 4 inches by 4 feet 4 inches by 4 feet 7 inches and is faced with 2 inches of granite. Wikipedia gives the same two-tier description. A plaque carries the closing lines of Lincoln’s Second Inaugural Address of 4 March 1865, beginning with the words with malice towards none.

The nomination says this was the first statue of Abraham Lincoln erected beside and dedicated to the Lincoln Highway. Wikipedia says the same, and says the figure is a replica of W. Granville Hastings’ statue in Cincinnati. The nomination quotes donor E.B. Wilson, whose full name it gives as Edmund Burk Wilson, saying the sculptor was an Englishman named Hastings who came to this country in 1914, and that this was the third replica: the first in front of the courthouse at Trenton, New Jersey, and a second he had forgotten. This guide keeps the Cincinnati account and Wilson’s Trenton account side by side.

Wilson and his wife, Minnie, decided on the gift after the courthouse was finished in the summer of 1917. The Greene County Board of Supervisors agreed to pay half the cost of the base. Bureau Brothers of Philadelphia cast the bronze and included the plaque. The statue was listed on the National Register on 29 March 1993, reference 93000165. Wikipedia places it on less than one acre and notes that it also contributes to the Jefferson Square Commercial Historic District.

Questions

Where is the statue, and how large is it?

It stands at Lincoln Way and Chestnut Street in Jefferson, on the south side of the courthouse square. The National Register nomination and Wikipedia both call the bronze life size. The nomination describes the left arm holding a paper and the right arm extended.

When was it dedicated, and is it listed?

The nomination dates the dedication to 22 September 1918 and says the concrete base was replaced in 1964. The National Register listing date is 29 March 1993, reference 93000165.
