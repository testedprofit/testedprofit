Karl Richter memorial

The National War Memorial Registry lists a statue of 1st Lt. Karl W. Richter at Maxwell Air Park on Maxwell Air Force Base in Montgomery, Alabama, zip code 36112, and it lists access as military base. Maxwell's base-access page explains how a visitor pass works.

Plan your visit

Address
Maxwell Air Park, Maxwell Air Force Base, Montgomery, Alabama 36112, as the registry prints it.
Parking
The access page says a guest who will drive on the installation must present vehicle registration and proof of insurance when obtaining a visitor pass. It does not name a lot at Air Park.
Hours
The Visitor Control Center is Monday through Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., and closed on weekends and federal holidays. The Maxwell Boulevard Gate is listed as open 24 hours. The statue page does not add separate viewing hours.
Accessibility
People without a DoD-approved credential need a visitor pass. The page requires a REAL ID-compliant credential for a pass as of 7 May 2025, sponsorship by a DoD-affiliated person, and a background check.
Best time
The access page says peak traffic at the gates is about 7:45 to 8:15 a.m. and asks drivers to plan for delays. The Visitor Control Center is closed on weekends.

What the registry lists

The registry location line reads Maxwell Air Park, Maxwell Air Force Base, city Montgomery, Alabama, zip code 36112. The type line is statues, war or military related. Access is military base. The war commemorated is the Vietnam War. The inscription block, as the registry prints it, names 1st Lt. Karl W. Richter, killed in action 28 July 1967, North Vietnam, and then the Isaiah 6:8 lines: Whom shall I send, and who will go for us? Here am I. Send me.

The listing credits the photograph to William Fischer Jr. and says the submission came through HMDB.org. It does not name a sculptor, a metal, or a height. Those fields stay blank here.

Visitor access on Maxwell

Maxwell Air Force Base publishes a base-access fact sheet for Maxwell and Gunter Annex. It says that as of 7 May 2025, visitors must present a REAL ID-compliant credential to be issued a pass. A REAL ID-compliant driver's license is the card with a star. A U.S. passport or passport card also qualifies. People without a DoD-approved access credential get a visitor pass at the Visitor Control Center.

The Visitor Control Center is next to the Maxwell Boulevard Gate. Hours are Monday through Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., closed weekends and federal holidays. The phone is 334-953-4283. When the center is closed, the fact sheet says passes can be acquired at the Maxwell Boulevard Main Gate or at the Cong. Dickenson Gate at Gunter Annex.

A visitor pass, as the sheet lists it, needs a valid credential, sponsorship by a DoD-affiliated individual, and a background check. A guest who will drive on the installation must also present vehicle registration and proof of insurance. All vehicle operators must present a valid credential at entry. The sheet warns of delays at peak hours, about 7:45 to 8:15 a.m., and lists the Maxwell Boulevard Gate as open 24 hours. The Day Street Gate is listed as closed.

Foreign national visitors have a separate path on the same sheet: a Foreign National Installation Access Request emailed to 42sfs.vcc.max@us.af.mil at least three weeks ahead, a background check, and a sponsor who meets the visitor at the Visitor Control Center. The sheet also says Uber may operate on Maxwell and Gunter only with drivers who already hold a DBIDS credential, and that passengers still need their own valid DoD credential. That ride-share note is about entering the base, not about a shuttle to the statue.

How to read the pin

OpenStreetMap's result for Maxwell Air Force Base is the pin on this page, at 32.3819133, -86.3533167. The registry's place name is Maxwell Air Park on that base. Use the base-access rules before you treat the pin as a public park entrance. Montgomery guides for the Hank Williams statue, the Confederate Monument on Capitol Hill, and Court Square Fountain are separate downtown stops, not stops inside the gate.

Questions

Where is the Karl Richter memorial, and who can visit?

The National War Memorial Registry places the statue at Maxwell Air Park on Maxwell Air Force Base in Montgomery and lists access as military base. Maxwell's fact sheet says visitors without a DoD credential need a visitor pass, a REAL ID-compliant document as of 7 May 2025, a DoD-affiliated sponsor, and a background check.

What does the registry say was inscribed?

It prints that 1st Lt. Karl W. Richter was killed in action on 28 July 1967 in North Vietnam, and it quotes Isaiah 6:8, Whom shall I send, and who will go for us? Here am I. Send me.

When is the Visitor Control Center open?

The base-access sheet says Monday through Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., closed weekends and federal holidays, next to the Maxwell Boulevard Gate.
