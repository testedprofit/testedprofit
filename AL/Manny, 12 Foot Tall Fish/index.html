Manny the bass

Outdoor Alabama, in a February 1, 2018 story by David Rainer of the Alabama Department of Conservation and Natural Resources, says a 12-foot-tall replica of a largemouth bass was dedicated the previous week in downtown Eufaula. Mayor Jack Tibbs said the fish is also 5 feet wide. The story gives the photo stop as 404 East Broad Street.

The name

The city held a naming contest. Tibbs said more than 40 names were submitted, and the winner was Manny, for Tom Mann, whom the story calls Eufaula’s most famous fisherman. The town has long claimed the title “Big Bass Capital of the World.” A grant from the Alabama Tourism Department paid for the replica, which was built in Dothan. The same dedication included a historic marker about Mann. The story says he died in 2005, that his lure company was Mann Bait Company, and that his daughter, Sharon Mann Dixon, was at the ceremony.
