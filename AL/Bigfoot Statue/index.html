Evergreen, Big Foot Capital of Alabama

The address of this page is /AL/Bigfoot%20Statue/. The public account describes a city name, not a statue.

The council vote

WSFA’s February 10, 2017 story says Evergreen, already designated the Collard Green Capital of Alabama, was now officially the Big Foot Capital of Alabama. Councilman Luther Upton told WSFA the idea followed the city’s Collard Green Festival on the second weekend of January. During that festival, Donald McDonald, host of Killing Bigfoot, and others spoke with officials and residents about sightings. McDonald told WSFA there had been numerous reports of Big Foot sightings within a 20-mile radius of the city.

Upton then asked the council to name the city the official Big Foot Capital of Alabama. WSFA says the council took a vote and it passed unanimously. The story says officials were working with lawmakers so the state would recognize the name as well. It does not say that a later state resolution passed.

Last updated 21 October 2026.

Questions

Did Evergreen’s council name the city a Bigfoot capital?

WSFA reported on February 10, 2017 that the Evergreen City Council’s vote passed unanimously and that the city was officially the Big Foot Capital of Alabama.

Does this page document a Bigfoot statue?

No. The WSFA story describes the name and the council vote. It does not describe a statue, a height, or a highway exit.
