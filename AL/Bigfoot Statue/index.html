Bigfoot statue in Evergreen

The Bigfoot statue is a roadside landmark in Evergreen, Alabama. It is the figure travelers look for in the town that calls itself the Big Foot Capital of Alabama.

WSFA's February 10, 2017 story says Evergreen, already designated the Collard Green Capital of Alabama, was then officially the Big Foot Capital of Alabama. Councilman Luther Upton told WSFA the idea followed the city's Collard Green Festival on the second weekend of January. During that festival, Donald McDonald, host of Killing Bigfoot, and others spoke with officials and residents about sightings. McDonald told WSFA there had been numerous reports of Big Foot sightings within a 20-mile radius of the city. Upton asked the council to name the city the official Big Foot Capital of Alabama. WSFA says the vote passed unanimously. The story says officials were working with lawmakers so the state would recognize the name as well.

The Bigfoot statue is the roadside landmark in that town. Evergreen's capital title, the festival, and the sightings McDonald described are the public story WSFA printed around the name. The statue stands in the town that carries both the collard-green title and the Big Foot title.
