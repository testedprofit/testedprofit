Bo Jackson statue

Ken Bjorge’s gallery says he began work in 2010 on monuments of Auburn University’s three Heisman Trophy winners and a bust of John Heisman, who coached at Auburn from 1895 to 1899. The completed monuments of Pat Sullivan (1971), Bo Jackson (1985), and Cam Newton (2010), and the bust of John Heisman, were unveiled on April 14, 2012, on the east side of Jordan-Hare Stadium. The gallery says they are permanently located there.

Size

The gallery caption for Bo Jackson reads “one and one half life-size.” The same captions use that phrase for the Sullivan and Newton monuments and for the Heisman bust. The gallery describes Bjorge’s work as images in bronze. It does not give this monument a weight, a street address, or a yardage total.

The gallery prints a note from Jeffery Steele, associate athletic director for facilities and operations at Auburn, about the design of the statues. It does not add a different location.

What this guide dropped

An earlier version said the bronze weighed 1,900 pounds and gave rushing statistics. The gallery page does not state a weight or a yardage figure, so those numbers were removed. A 2012 Wareagle Reader account of the unveiling, and the sculptor’s separate studio page for this monument, did not return readable copies when checked, so they are not linked.

Questions

Where is the Bo Jackson statue, and when was it unveiled?

Ken Bjorge’s gallery says it was unveiled on April 14, 2012, on the east side of Jordan-Hare Stadium at Auburn, with the Pat Sullivan and Cam Newton monuments and a bust of John Heisman.

What size does the sculptor’s gallery give?

The caption calls the Bo Jackson monument one and one half life-size. The gallery does not give a weight.
