The Big Peanut

WTVY’s September 5, 2023 story says the National Peanut Festival started in 1938, that Dr. George Washington Carver was the first guest speaker, and that the fairgrounds are marked by a 24-foot-tall peanut statue built in 2015. WTVY calls the festival the largest of its kind in the nation.

The fairgrounds

Visit Dothan lists the National Peanut Festival Fairgrounds at 5622 Hwy 231 South and gives the phone number (334) 793-4323. The venue hosts the National Peanut Festival and other events. Visit Dothan says it has a covered stage amphitheater with field seating, a covered arena with bleachers, and several commercial buildings.

Festival history

The festival’s own history page says the first festival was in 1938, with Carver as guest speaker and Harry P. Hall as the first president. It was not held during World War II. The Jaycees and the Chamber of Commerce brought it back in 1947. In 1993 the festival bought 150 acres on Highway 231 South. In 1999 it moved to its current location on Highway 231 South.

Questions

Where is the Big Peanut?

WTVY says a 24-foot peanut statue built in 2015 marks the National Peanut Festival Fairgrounds. Visit Dothan lists that fairgrounds at 5622 Hwy 231 South in Dothan.

When did the National Peanut Festival start?

The festival’s history page and WTVY both say the first festival was in 1938, with Dr. George Washington Carver as the guest speaker. It was not held during World War II and returned in 1947.
