Hank Williams statue

WSFA reported on October 17, 2016, that the Hank Williams statue which had overlooked Montgomery’s City Hall for 25 years was being moved. Steve Jones, director of general services for the city, said the statue had been underused where it was, and that more people would see it in the new place.

The new corner

WSFA says the city was moving the statue to Commerce Street, onto median work between Court Square Fountain and the tunnel that leads to Riverfront Park. Jones said the last piece of stamped-concrete median, between Bibb Street and Montgomery Street, had been finished about a month earlier, so a person at the fountain could look all the way to the tunnel. The statue would stand at the intersection of Commerce and Tallapoosa streets. Jones called that a first step toward a larger plan for the sidewalk.
