Angel of Hope

The Angel of Hope is at Westgate Park in Dothan. Rickey Stokes News, on December 2, 2025, says the statue is directly inside the park at the Choctaw Drive entrance, with HOPE engraved on the right wing.

The 2016 dedication

An Associated Press story carried by The News Courier on Monday, November 21, 2016, says an Angel of Hope was dedicated at Westgate Park the previous Sunday. Steve Hardwick organized the fundraising after local deaths. The statue follows The Christmas Box, the book and television movie by Richard Paul Evans. Evans commissioned a statue from the book and dedicated it in late 1994 at a cemetery in Salt Lake City. The News Courier story says other cities then wanted their own, and that the Dothan statue was the 136th erected in the United States, Canada, and Japan.

Mayor Mike Schmitz took part in the Sunday ceremony. The city commission and the Department of Leisure Services placed the memorial between Miracle Field, the tennis complex, the library, and the recreation center. Hardwick said a candlelight vigil would be held at the site every year on December 6 at 7 p.m. The December 2025 Rickey Stokes item also describes a December 6 vigil at 7 p.m. at the statue.
