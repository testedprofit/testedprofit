Deontay Wilder statue

The City of Tuscaloosa announced that on May 25, 2022, at 2 p.m., the city and Tuscaloosa Tourism and Sports would unveil a statue of Deontay Wilder by Tuscaloosa-based artist Caleb O’Connor. The permanent place is outside the Tuscaloosa Tourism and Sports building at 1900 Jack Warner Parkway. The announcement says Wilder, a Tuscaloosa native, held the World Boxing Council heavyweight title from 2015 to 2020, and that his bronze medal in the heavyweight division at the 2008 Olympics earned the nickname Bronze Bomber.

The unveiling

Alabama Public Radio, on May 25, 2022, reported that a bronze statue was unveiled at the city’s Tourism and Sports Bureau. It says O’Connor had been working on the statue since 2015, the year Wilder first won the WBC heavyweight title, and that Wilder made no changes to the design. The finished statue reflects a later hairstyle and a beard. Mayor Walt Maddox and trainer Jay Deas were there.

Questions

Where is the Deontay Wilder statue?

The City of Tuscaloosa says the permanent place is outside the Tuscaloosa Tourism and Sports building at 1900 Jack Warner Parkway. The unveiling was set for May 25, 2022, at 2 p.m.

Who made it, and why Bronze Bomber?

The city names sculptor Caleb O’Connor. It says the nickname comes from Wilder’s bronze medal at the 2008 Olympics. Alabama Public Radio calls the unveiled statue bronze.
