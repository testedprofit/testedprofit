Torii Square Park

Torii Square Park is in Westminster at 76th Avenue and Lowell Boulevard. A 1975 photograph in the Westminster Historical Society collection records the dedication.

1975 dedication

The record says the Westminster Rotary Club donated the funds to develop Torii Square Park in memory of Yuko Nakamura Kishimoto, a Rotary exchange student sponsored by the club. It names Ken Gay of the Rotary Club and Mayor Gil Bean at the podium. The photograph title is Dedication of Torii Square Park 76th Ave and Lowell Blvd.

What this guide does not claim

The museum record does not give a street number, park hours, a pet rule, or a description of a torii gate or cherry trees. A later city news story and a Colorado Community Media article were not linked here because those sites did not return a readable page when checked on 21 October 2026. Details that appeared only in those accounts were removed.
