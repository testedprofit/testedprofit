The Newsboy Statue in Golden, Colorado

The statue in Golden, Colorado that visitors mean by the Newsboy is a bronze by James Muir on the northwest corner of the Washington Avenue bridge over Clear Creek. A 2018 Visit Golden public-art brochure lists it as placed on 5 December 2003. Golden, CO searches for a downtown bronze land on this bridge corner. It is outdoors and free to look at from the sidewalk.

The sculpture and what it commemorates

James Muir calls The Newsboy a life-size bronze allegory, 70 inches high, in an edition of 12. His description is a newsboy from the past heralding a new era: a little David armed with the pebble of truth to face the Goliath of falsehood. The slingshot in the hip pocket is the warning that well-aimed truth is accurate. The front page shows the newsboy holding up the headline Truth, and Muir says that picture mirrors the theme the way a pebble sends ripples across a pond.

That is what the figure commemorates: the newsboy as a carrier of truth, not a portrait of one named carrier. In Golden, Colorado, the bronze stands where Washington Avenue crosses Clear Creek, so the paper in his hands is the detail to look for on the bridge.

The 2018 Visit Golden brochure is the placement record: Newsboy, NW corner of Washington Ave. Bridge, artist James Muir, placed 12/05/2003. OpenStreetMap records an artwork node of that name at 39.7568894, -105.2228976, just northwest of the bridge. Use the bridge corner on Washington Avenue. The statue is the one on that corner, over the creek.

Washington Avenue and other Golden statues

Clear Creek runs under the span, so you are already at the water. The Golden Visitor Center at 1010 Washington Ave is a short walk south, in ZIP 80401. The same 2018 brochure puts Sleeping Bear at the visitor center, 10th and Washington, and Howdy Folks at 10th and Washington. Those three bronzes make a short walk on one street.

CPR News has photographed the Golden Newsboy with the headline Truth, and it reports that the Golden Civic Foundation began giving bronzes to the city in the 1990s, with a public art commission organized in 2013. The Newsboy's 2003 date in the brochure is earlier than that commission. Golden, Colorado also has a separate dinosaur page on this site, Triceratops Sculptures near Golden.

Denver bronzes are a short drive east: the Bronco Buster Statue in Denver's Civic Center Park, Botero Denver Man and Woman Sculpture, and the Justina Ford Lady Doctor Statue in Denver. Use Denver Landmark Guides for the metro set, or go Back to Colorado for the state list.

Questions

Where is the statue in Golden, Colorado?

The Newsboy Statue is on the northwest corner of the Washington Avenue bridge over Clear Creek in downtown Golden, CO. A 2018 Visit Golden brochure lists it there, placed on 5 December 2003.

Who made the Newsboy Statue, and what does it commemorate?

James Muir. He describes the figure as a newsboy from the past, a little David armed with truth against falsehood. The paper's headline is Truth, and a slingshot in the hip pocket stands for well-aimed truth. He lists the bronze as life-size, 70 inches high, in an edition of 12.

When was the Newsboy placed in Golden?

The 2018 Visit Golden public-art brochure lists Newsboy, northwest corner of Washington Ave. Bridge, artist James Muir, placed 12/05/2003.

Is the Newsboy Statue free to see?

Yes. It is outdoor public art on the sidewalk along Washington Avenue, free to view.

What else is nearby in Golden, Colorado?

The same brochure maps Sleeping Bear at the Golden Visitor Center at 10th and Washington, and Howdy Folks at 10th and Washington. The visitor center is at 1010 Washington Ave. Clear Creek runs under the bridge.
