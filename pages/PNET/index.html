What is $PNET

Profitnet is the name of this project and of the site at testedprofit.com. On Algorand, the asset name is ProfitNet and the unit name is PNET. People search for it as the PNET token, the Profitnet Algorand token, or PNET ASA 3169177585. Those names refer to the same asset.

The asset was created on 12 August 2025 in round 52678300. Its on-chain URL field is https://testedprofit.com. Pera’s public asset record lists the ASA as verified and names the project ProfitNet, with the project URL on this domain and the X account @TestedProfit. That verification is a listing status. It is not a promise about price or utility.

The total supply is 100,000,000 PNET. Six decimals means the chain stores that supply as 100,000,000,000,000 base units. Because the manager address is empty, that configuration cannot be edited to raise the total. Circulating supply is different: it changes when tokens move, including to any address someone treats as a burn address. This page does not print a circulating figure. Open Pera Explorer or Vestige for the live account balances.

The tokenomics page is where this site lists vaults and allocation notes, including vaults it describes as a 25 million PNET lock and a 15 million PNET lock, plus liquidity-pair vaults. Use the Vestige links on that page to read each vault. Those amounts are not repeated here as audited totals. The roadmap is the project’s plan page. Read it as a plan, not as a schedule of returns.

AlgoFlow is the Algorand tool suite published with Profitnet. It includes a wallet inspector, a multisender, an ASA launchpad, AlgoSocial, and a TestNet NFT studio. $PNET is the project token. This page does not say you must hold it to open those tools. PNET Genesis, the bonding-curve launch flow, is offline for review.

How to get $PNET

There is no sale on this page. These are the ordinary Algorand steps for an ASA. Confirm the asset ID before you opt in or swap. This page does not quote a price.

  1. 1

    Use an Algorand wallet

    The tokenomics page links Pera and Defly. Either can hold an Algorand Standard Asset. You need a small ALGO balance for fees and for the opt-in transaction. This page does not state a fee amount.

  2. 2

    Opt in to ASA 3169177585

    An Algorand account cannot receive this asset until it opts in. In the wallet, add ASA 3169177585 and confirm the name ProfitNet and the unit name PNET before you sign. Opt-in does not buy tokens. It only allows the account to hold them.

  3. 3

    Swap only after you confirm a pool

    This site already links Tinyman and Pact. Search ASA 3169177585 on the exchange, check that the pool is the asset you expect, and read the price there. Tinyman’s public asset index lists PNET. A listing is not a promise that a pool has the depth you want. Vestige is the chart link already used on this page.

  4. 4

    Or receive a transfer

    After the opt-in, another account can send PNET to you. The holder leaderboard lists large PNET addresses. It is a ranking page, not a marketplace.

What you can do with it

You can hold the ASA, transfer it, and trade it where a pool exists. On this site you can also read the records below and open Profitnet’s AlgoFlow tools. Those tools are separate pages. Unless a tool page says otherwise, holding $PNET is not a requirement to open them, and opening them is not a payout.

AlgoFlow

The Algorand tool suite: launchpad, multisend, wallet inspector, AlgoSocial, and related utilities. ShapeMap on that suite uses ASA 3169177585 as its default read-only PNET scan.

Wallet Inspector

Look up an Algorand address: ALGO balance, ASA holdings, and flags the page describes for clawback-enabled assets. The inspector page says no wallet connection is required.

Multisend

Send ALGO or an Algorand Standard Asset to more than one address in one signed group. It is a sending tool, not a faucet for $PNET.

ASA launchpad

Mint a new Algorand Standard Asset and choose its own name and supply. That creates a different asset. It is not a way to buy $PNET.

AlgoSocial
