The Einstein bench

Vail Daily reported on 9 October 2014 that Sitzmark Vail, through Bob and Helen Fritch, donated Gary Lee Price's bronze Einstein, from his Great Contributors series, to the Town of Vail Art in Public Places collection. The sculpture had already stood on Gore Creek Drive for many years. Molly Eppard, the program coordinator, said that unless something unforeseen happened it would stay in front of the Sitzmark. That article described the town collection as more than 40 works.

Discover Vail's Einstein Bench story still places the bronze in Art in Public Places, invites visitors to sit on the bench, and also says the collection contains more than 40 works. Discover Vail's Art in Public Places page is a separate tourism page. It says the program includes more than 70 pieces and names a bronze Albert Einstein relaxing on a bench. Those two counts belong to those two pages.

Sitzmark Vail publishes its address as 183 Gore Creek Drive, Vail, Colorado 81657. The visitor guide uses that address and the hotel's map pin. It does not claim a separate survey of the pedestal.

Gary Lee Price's studio page lists two 2005 bronze editions, one 52 inches high and one 18 inches high. It does not say which edition is the cast on Gore Creek Drive. This directory does not copy the studio's prices.
