Barack Obama Statue on San Juan's Walkway of the Presidents

Yes. There is a Barack Obama statue in Puerto Rico. It stands in San Juan on the Walkway of the Presidents, the Paseo de los Presidentes, in the landscaped park on the south side of the Capitol. The walk honors sitting United States presidents who visited the island. Obama's life-size bronze, unveiled on 20 February 2012, is the newest of those figures.

Plan your visit

Address
South grounds of the Capitol of Puerto Rico, between Juan Ponce de León Avenue and Paseo Covadonga, from Calle Esteves to Calle Gral. Contreras, San Juan.
Getting there
Face the rear of the capitol and walk the line of monuments from east to west. The walkway sits after the Carlos Luis Cáceres Collazo Monument and before the Recipients of Medal of Honor Monument.
Best time
Morning light on the south façade makes the row of monuments easy to follow. The grounds are an outdoor walk, so bring sun cover on a bright day.
Obama
His bronze is on the Avenue of Heroes, beside Gerald Ford, with Lyndon B. Johnson a few steps away. It was unveiled on 20 February 2012.
Tip
Look for Franklin D. Roosevelt seated in a wheelchair, with his dog Fala. A separate square holds a bronze of John the Baptist and the coats of arms of Puerto Rico's 78 municipalities. Stay on the south park if you are looking for the presidents.

Who the walkway remembers

The Walkway of the Presidents, the Paseo de los Presidentes, is the capitol grounds' memorial to presidents who came to Puerto Rico, one stop in a longer east-to-west park of sculptures and plaques.

From east to west the prominent monuments run: Rafael Hernández Marín, Santiago Iglesias Pantin, the Altar to the Nation, Carlos Luis Cáceres Collazo, the Walkway of the Presidents, the Recipients of the Medal of Honor, the World War I Monument, the Memorial Monument, the Holocaust Memorial, the Teachers Monument, Lions' Plaza, the Police Monument, the Puerto Rican Woman Monument, and Ramón Mellado Parsons. Use that order as a walking list. If you have just passed Caceres Collazo, the presidents are next.

The presidents on the path

The statues honor every sitting United States president who has visited the island, a line that runs back to Theodore Roosevelt. On the walk you can pick out Theodore Roosevelt, Franklin D. Roosevelt seated with his dog Fala, Gerald Ford, Lyndon B. Johnson, and Barack Obama. Obama and Johnson were unveiled together on 20 February 2012, Presidents Day. Obama was the ninth sitting president to make that visit.

Why it matters

The capitol complex is the seat of Puerto Rico's legislature. The presidents' walk is the stop that ties visits from Washington to the island's own ground, set between local political figures, war memorials, and civic groups.

The square with John the Baptist is a different space. Its bronze stands on a low semicircular pedestal carrying the coats of arms of the 78 municipalities. Stay on the elongated park behind the capitol when the presidents are the goal.

What to see nearby

From the Memorial Monument, the park connects toward the San Juan Cruise Port on San Juan Bay by way of Calle Gral. Contreras. The capitol's south façade is the backdrop: dome, columns, and the park in front of it.

A simple route

Start at the east end near Calle Esteves, with Juan Ponce de León Avenue on your right and Paseo Covadonga on your left as you face west. Count the monuments until you reach the Walkway of the Presidents. After the Medal of Honor monument you are past it. Obama is on that presidents' stretch, beside Ford.

The capitol behind you

The building on the north side of the park is El Capitolio, seat of Puerto Rico's Legislative Assembly. It stands on San Juan Islet, just outside the walls of Old San Juan, a white marble capitol in a Neoclassical Beaux-Arts design by Rafael Carmoega, built from 1921 to 1929. It has been on the National Register of Historic Places since 1977. The park is the civic lawn in front of that south façade.

Give the Roosevelt bronze a minute when you reach the presidents. He is seated, one hand on the wheel of the chair, with Fala at his feet. The Commons photograph of that figure names sculptor Salvador Rivera Cardona. Roosevelt is one president on the line, not the only name on the lawn.

The Barack Obama statue

Barack Obama's bronze is the newest president on this path, and it is the Barack Obama statue in Puerto Rico. On 20 February 2012, Presidents Day, Puerto Rico unveiled life-size bronzes of Obama and Lyndon B. Johnson on the Avenue of Heroes, the broad walk along the south side of the capitol. Primera Hora reported that unveiling on the Paseo de los Presidentes. The two new figures were part of a million-dollar project to improve that avenue.

Look for him beside Gerald Ford. Johnson stands a few steps away. The marker at Obama's statue calls him the 44th president and the first African American to hold the office, and it records the visit a visitor can match to the bronze: on 11 July 2011 he arrived at Luis Muñoz Marín Airport on Air Force One with Resident Commissioner Pedro Pierluisi, addressed people in Puerto Rico, and went on to La Fortaleza, where Governor Luis Fortuño was waiting with other officials.

Questions

Is there a Barack Obama statue in Puerto Rico?

Yes. A life-size bronze was unveiled on 20 February 2012 on the Walkway of the Presidents, also called the Paseo de los Presidentes, on the south side of the Capitol in San Juan. It stands on the Avenue of Heroes beside Gerald Ford, with Lyndon B. Johnson a few steps away. The walk honors sitting U.S. presidents who visited the island, back to Theodore Roosevelt. Obama was the ninth to make that visit. The marker at his statue dates the arrival to 11 July 2011.

Where is the Walkway of the Presidents?

On the south grounds of the Capitol of Puerto Rico in San Juan, in the landscaped park between Juan Ponce de León Avenue and Paseo Covadonga.

Which presidents' statues are on the walkway?

The walk honors sitting United States presidents who visited Puerto Rico, a line that runs back to Theodore Roosevelt. The bronzes include Theodore Roosevelt, Franklin D. Roosevelt seated with his dog Fala, Gerald Ford, Lyndon B. Johnson, and Barack Obama, unveiled with Johnson on 20 February 2012.

Who made the Roosevelt figure on the walkway?

The Wikimedia Commons file of Franklin D. Roosevelt and Fala names sculptor Salvador Rivera Cardona. That credit belongs to the Roosevelt bronze in the photograph.

How do I find the Obama statue among the other monuments?

Walk east to west. The Walkway of the Presidents comes after the Carlos Luis Cáceres Collazo Monument and before the Recipients of Medal of Honor Monument. Obama is on that presidents' stretch, beside Ford.
