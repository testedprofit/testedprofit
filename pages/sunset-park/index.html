Sunset Park

Sunset Park is a City of Westminster playground at 4321 W. 78th Ave. The city's playground inventory lists the year built as 1996 and the district as South.

Playground inventory

The inventory has three features for this park name. Two are play surfaces: engineered wood fiber, recorded as EWF, and a unitary surface. Both have the improvement note Replace Sand / EWF. A third feature is the boundary. The project-completed field is blank on all three, so the inventory does not record a completion date for that surface work.

What the inventory does not list

It does not list hours, a star rating, picnic shelters, or walking paths. An earlier version of this page said the park was open 24 hours and rated 4.1 stars. Those claims are not in the city inventory, so they were removed.

Questions

Where is Sunset Park, and when was it built?

The City of Westminster playground inventory lists Sunset Park at 4321 W. 78th Ave, year built 1996, in the south district.

What playground surface does the inventory list?

It lists engineered wood fiber (EWF) and a unitary surface, plus a boundary feature. The improvement field reads Replace Sand / EWF, and the project-completed field is blank.
