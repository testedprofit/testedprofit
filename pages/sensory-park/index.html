Westminster Sensory ParkThe city's first completely accessible playground

Westminster Sensory Park is a city playground near Wadsworth Boulevard and West 103rd Avenue, built for children of all abilities. The city's playground inventory lists it at 10376 Wadsworth Blvd., built in 2000, in the west district. Parks spokeswoman Angela Simental called it the city's first completely accessible play area, built with Children's Hospital Colorado.

What you'll see

In a July 22, 2020 Colorado Community Media story, Westminster parks spokeswoman Angela Simental said Sensory Park is ADA-compliant and therapy-focused, and still fun for all kids. She called it the city's first completely accessible play area, built in cooperation with Children's Hospital Colorado, and said it is easy to reach from the local trail system. The address she gave was 7577 W 103rd Ave.

5280's April 16, 2024 guide says the playground has been popular since 1998, when Children's Hospital approached the city with funds and a grant paid for the rest. It describes supportive swings, an undulating walkway for wheelchairs, steps and paths painted for people with visual impairments, sand pits, and brightly colored decorative fish. 5280 prints the address as 7355 W 103rd Ave. and says the park is a few miles from Children's Hospital Colorado's North Campus.

The City of Westminster playground inventory is the address and year used on the map: Sensory Park, 10376 Wadsworth Blvd., year built 2000, west district. It lists an EWF surface and a unitary surface, with a recorded improvement "Replace Sand / EWF" completed in August 2016. A January 19, 2012 Colorado Community Media report, citing the city, also said Westminster and Children's Hospital built the playground in 2000. That 2012 story placed a hospital therapy center at 7577 W 103rd Ave., across from the park, and said the center was moving.

Visiting tips

  • If the street number disagrees: Use the playground at Wadsworth and W 103rd. The city inventory says 10376 Wadsworth Blvd. A 2020 city spokeswoman said 7577 W 103rd Ave. 5280 in 2024 printed 7355 W 103rd Ave.
  • What is built for access: Simental said it is ADA-compliant. 5280 describes supportive swings, a wheelchair walkway, and painted steps and paths.
  • Restrooms: A 2018 visitor write-up reports no restroom on site. That is a visitor report, not a city statement.
  • Fee: No admission fee appears in the city inventory or the magazine guides. Hours were not posted in those sources.

Westminster Sensory Park FAQ

Where is Westminster Sensory Park?

The City of Westminster playground inventory lists Sensory Park at 10376 Wadsworth Blvd., in the west district. The playground footprint on that map sits near Wadsworth Boulevard and West 103rd Avenue. A 2020 parks spokeswoman gave 7577 W 103rd Ave., and 5280's 2024 guide printed 7355 W 103rd Ave.

Is Westminster Sensory Park free and accessible?

No admission fee is listed. Parks spokeswoman Angela Simental told Colorado Community Media in 2020 that it is ADA-compliant and is the city's first completely accessible play area, built in cooperation with Children's Hospital Colorado. 5280 describes supportive swings, a wheelchair walkway, and paths painted for visual impairments.

When was Westminster Sensory Park built?

The city inventory lists the year built as 2000. A 2012 news report, citing the city, also says Westminster and Children's Hospital built it in 2000. 5280 says the hospital approached the city with funds in 1998 and a grant paid for the rest of the project. The grant amount was not stated.

What playground surface does Sensory Park have?

The city inventory lists an EWF surface, engineered wood fiber, and a unitary surface, and records a Replace Sand / EWF project completed in August 2016. 5280's 2024 article still describes sand pits, along with brightly colored decorative fish.

Does Westminster Sensory Park have restrooms, water play, or a static-free slide?

The sources checked do not describe a water-play area or a static-free slide at this park. A 2018 visitor write-up reports no restrooms. Posted hours were not found in the city inventory or the magazine guides.
