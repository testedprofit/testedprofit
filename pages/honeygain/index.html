Honeygain review

Join Honeygain

This is our referral link; we may earn a bonus if you join.

Honeygain's features page says the app pays you for sharing unused internet bandwidth. While you stay connected, the device joins a network that gathers public web data, including SEO insights, market trends, and ad verification. The page says private data is never accessed or stored, and that the app runs in the background.

Credits and devices

The features page says you earn credits for every megabyte shared, and that a steadier connection shares more. It lists three factors: how long the device stays connected, how much data is needed in your region, and how many devices you connect on different networks. At setup it names mobile, desktop, or tablet. Its FAQ names Windows, macOS, Android, and Linux. The homepage names Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android.

On speed, the features FAQ says Honeygain uses a small portion of unused bandwidth and never runs when the connection is actively in use, and that most users do not notice a difference during regular activities. The same FAQ says you can set a daily traffic limit or pause the app.

Payout figures the site prints

The features page sets the payout threshold at 20,000 credits, which it equates to $20, and names two methods: PayPal and JumpToken (JMPT), which it calls a crypto-based alternative. The FAQ on that same page says that once you earn $20 worth of credits you can request PayPal or a gift card, and that most payouts are processed within a few business days.

The homepage says you can take earnings via PayPal at $20 or withdraw crypto sooner, and it also says a payout can be PayPal or crypto once you reach the payout minimum. The hero on that page says join now and get a $2 starting gift. A separate homepage card says referrals pay up to $5 when a friend reaches the $20 payout threshold.

The features footer calls Honeygain the first app that pays users for sharing an internet connection, in USD or crypto. A homepage section about a Tipalti award says the service has over 60 million users. The features page says millions of users.
