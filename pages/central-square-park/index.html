Central Plaza Park

The City of Westminster calls this site Central Plaza Park. The facility page places it at 5845 W. 89th Avenue, Westminster, CO 80031, and classifies it as an urban plaza of 1.32 acres. This guide keeps the existing URL, which says central-square-park.

What the facility page lists

  • One pavilion
  • 14 picnic tables
  • A restroom
  • A drinking fountain
  • Adirondack chairs
  • A lighted granite water feature

Water feature hours

The facility page marks the water feature closed for maintenance. It still lists the season as Memorial Day through Labor Day, with operational hours of 7 am to 11 pm, and says the fountain may be shut off for weather, climate, maintenance, or other operational concerns.

The city's park pavilions page lists the water features at Central Plaza Park, Irving Street Park, McFall Park, and Westminster Station Park and Nature Playground as Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day, 9 am to 7 pm every day. It also says those fountains may be shut off for weather, drought, maintenance, or operations, and that dogs are not allowed in the water play features.

The two city pages do not print the same clock times. This guide reports both.

Removed from this page

An earlier version called the plaza Center Park, posted a 4.0-star rating, said construction began in May 2024 and would finish in late 2025, and listed a $10.7 million budget. The facility page checked on 21 October 2026 does not say any of that. McFall Park, not this plaza, is the park the city says was formerly Westminster Center Park. That is a different facility.

Last updated 21 October 2026.

Questions

What does the city call the park at 5845 W. 89th Avenue?

The facility page calls it Central Plaza Park, an urban plaza of 1.32 acres in Westminster, CO 80031.

When is the water feature open?

The facility page says Memorial Day through Labor Day, with operational hours of 7 am to 11 pm, and it marks the feature closed for maintenance. The park pavilions page says the Central Plaza Park water feature runs Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day, 9 am to 7 pm. Both pages say the fountain may be shut off for weather or maintenance.
