EarnApp review

Join EarnApp

This is our referral link; we may earn a bonus if you join.

EarnApp is software from Bright Data. The user agreement says it uses your IP address and free resources to download a few web pages in the background from well-known sites, in a way that does not materially interrupt you. Those pages go to Bright Data's customers, who use them to improve databases and to offer products, services, and pricing.

Credits and EarnApp Plus

Enabling EarnApp adds the device to the Bright Data network. The agreement says EarnApp changes network settings on the device and calculates credits with a proprietary algorithm. Credits, if Bright Data verifies them, may be redeemed for value. The agreement places the rates on the EarnApp dashboard.

EarnApp Plus is a separate mode. The agreement says it uses a real Google Chrome browser to download a few web pages in the background, and that it is a fixed-paid plan. EarnApp Plus is outside the referral program. Commissions in that mode do not apply as referrals.

Referrals and which devices qualify

The referral program pays for qualified installations: a new download by someone you referred. Those earnings are added to the EarnApp balance and show on the dashboard. The agreement does not print a commission percentage.

EarnApp is for personal devices in everyday use, including personal computers, mobile devices, and Smart TVs. The agreement says hosting services, including home servers, and other non-personal devices are a violation of the terms. It also says you cannot use EarnApp if a law bars it, or if your internet or mobile provider's agreement bars sharing your traffic, IP, and device resources.

The EarnApp homepage

The EarnApp homepage, copyrighted 2026 by Bright Data Ltd., is an offers page. It says the Earn page lists tasks, most of them under 10 minutes, from companies promoting apps, games, and products. The same page says users are not allowed to use multiple accounts, complete offers on behalf of other users, or utilize VPNs, VPS, or virtual machine software. The footer lists Bright Data Ltd. at 4 Hamahshev St., Netanya 4250713, Israel.
