Oakhurst II field in Westminster

Oakhurst II is a multi-use grass field on the City of Westminster rental list, named with Oakhurst I, Cotton Creek, Hampshire, Nottingham, Ryan, Sherwood, and Westfield. The city mows these fields. This is an independent Profitnet guide to that field and to the two Oakhurst parks you can walk to, not a city parks page.

The two Oakhurst parks

Oakhurst Park East
9255 Ammons Street, Broomfield, Colorado 80020. Neighborhood park, 2.7 acres. Multi-use turf, one barbecue grill, seven picnic tables, a portable restroom, parking, and a paved path. Open space along Big Dry Creek and the Niver Canal, and trails on Big Dry Creek and Farmer's Highline Canal.
Oakhurst Park West
9311 Lark Bunting Drive, Westminster, Colorado 80021. Community park, 5.86 acres. Multi-use turf, a full basketball court, one sand volleyball court, a playground, one pavilion, 20 picnic tables, three grills, a drinking fountain, a portable restroom, and parking. Trails on Big Dry Creek and Farmer's High Line Canal, and open space on the Niver Canal.
Parks office
City Hall, 4800 West 92nd Avenue, Westminster, Colorado 80031. Phone 303-658-2192, Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

What the rental list means

On the athletic-fields listing, Oakhurst I and Oakhurst II are grass fields, not diamond names. Renting one includes mowing. Paint and extra field prep are a separate charge, $65 a touch. The parks themselves are public. A group that wants the grass for a set time applies through athletic fields.

Applicants must be 21. Spring and summer 2027 applications open Monday, January 4. That season runs March 15 through July 31, 2027. An application is not a promise of the field. The city also asks for current liability insurance that names the City of Westminster as an additional insured. A nonprofit rate needs a 501(c)(3) already on file.

Posted rates for these multi-use grass fields:

  • Monday through Friday, 5 p.m. to dusk: $40 a night, or $35 for a nonprofit.
  • A weekday block, 8 a.m. to noon or 1 p.m. to 5 p.m.: $70. The full day, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.: $140.
  • Saturday or Sunday by the hour: $60, or $55 for a nonprofit.
  • Saturday or Sunday by the day: $210, or $160 for a nonprofit.

The same listing's detail for Oakhurst Park East, at 9255 Ammons Street, is a multi-use grass field that is mowed and dragged, with a portable restroom and parking. Oakhurst Park West, at 9311 Lark Bunting Drive, is mowed on a tiered schedule, with paint billed as a prep fee, a portable restroom, and parking.

East, the smaller park

Oakhurst Park East is the 2.7-acre neighborhood park. Westminster's facility page puts it at 9255 Ammons Street, Broomfield, Colorado 80020, on the edge of the two cities. You get one grill, seven picnic tables, a portable restroom, parking, and a paved walking path. The path meets open space along Big Dry Creek and the Niver Canal, and the regional trails on Big Dry Creek and Farmer's Highline Canal. The turf field is the flat grass in that park.

West, the community park

Oakhurst Park West is the larger stop, 5.86 acres at 9311 Lark Bunting Drive, Westminster, Colorado 80021. Besides the turf field it has play equipment, a full basketball court, a sand volleyball court, one pavilion, 20 picnic tables, three grills, a drinking fountain, a portable restroom, parking, and a paved path. The playground inventory records this address as Oakhurst Park Phase I, built in 1995, in the west district. The play surfaces are engineered wood fiber and a unitary surface. The sand-to-wood-fiber work was completed in May 2016.

A renovation is in design. On 12 October 2023 the parks department and the design team held a community barbecue. Neighbors voted to keep the tractor, and to add nature play, smart play, a full basketball court, and xeric plants. The city's goal is to start construction by summer 2027. Until that work begins, the amenities on the facility page are the ones in the park.

How a visit goes

For a walk, a picnic, or the playground, use the two addresses above. East is the canal-and-creek park with the smaller lawn. West is the one with the playground, the court, and the pavilion. For a reserved practice or a game on the grass, apply for Oakhurst I or Oakhurst II through the athletic-fields office and call 303-658-2192 on a weekday if you need the current form. The old /rotary-park/ address on this site opens this guide.

Questions

What is Oakhurst II?

Oakhurst II is a multi-use grass field on the City of Westminster athletic-fields list, grouped with Oakhurst I. The city mows the field. Paint and extra prep are a separate fee.

Where are the Oakhurst parks?

Oakhurst Park East is at 9255 Ammons Street, Broomfield, Colorado 80020, a 2.7-acre neighborhood park. Oakhurst Park West is at 9311 Lark Bunting Drive, Westminster, Colorado 80021, a 5.86-acre community park. Each has a multi-use turf field.

How do you reserve a field?

Applicants must be 21. Spring and summer 2027 applications open Monday, January 4, for the season from March 15 through July 31, 2027. A weeknight from 5 p.m. to dusk is $40, or $35 for a nonprofit. Call parks and recreation at 303-658-2192, weekdays 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.
