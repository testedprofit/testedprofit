Oakhurst II

The City of Westminster athletic-fields list names Oakhurst II as a multi-use grass field, grouped with Oakhurst I, Cotton Creek, Hampshire, Nottingham, Ryan, Sherwood, and Westfield. The page says those grass fields are mowed. It does not print a street address under the name Oakhurst II.

East and West are separate listings

The same athletic-fields page, and the city's facility pages, describe two other Oakhurst parks:

  • Oakhurst Park East, 9255 Ammons Street, Broomfield, CO 80020. The facility page calls it a 2.7-acre neighborhood park with a multi-use turf field, one barbecue grill, seven picnic tables, a portable restroom, parking, a paved walking path, and access to Big Dry Creek, the Niver Canal, and Farmer's Highline Canal.
  • Oakhurst Park West, 9311 Lark Bunting Drive, Westminster, CO 80021. The facility page calls it a 5.86-acre community park with play equipment, a full basketball court, a sand volleyball court, a pavilion, picnic tables, a portable restroom, and a multi-use turf field. The playground inventory lists this address as Oakhurst Park Phase I, year built 1995, with engineered wood fiber and unitary surfaces. It records Replace Sand / EWF as completed EWF 5/2016.

Oakhurst II, Oakhurst Park East, Oakhurst Park West, and Oakhurst Park Phase I are separate names. The city pages do not say which address is the field called Oakhurst II.

Questions

Does the city give Oakhurst II a street address?

The athletic-fields page names Oakhurst II as a multi-use grass field and does not print a separate street address for that name.

What are Oakhurst Park East and Oakhurst Park West?

They are separate city facility pages. East is 9255 Ammons Street, a 2.7-acre neighborhood park. West is 9311 Lark Bunting Drive, Westminster, a 5.86-acre community park. The playground inventory calls the Lark Bunting address Oakhurst Park Phase I.
