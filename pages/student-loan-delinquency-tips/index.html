Policy Alert: 2026 Deadlines

Student loan delinquency strategy

Cure Delinquency. Master the Shift.

The rules for student debt change on July 1, 2026. Don't let delinquency turn into default. Take strategic action today to protect your financial standing.

Stop The Clock

Ask your servicer which options apply when a payment is late. Forbearance can pause payments while the servicer has it in place. A missed-payment record is a separate question for that servicer.

  • A pause covers the period the servicer approves
  • Ask what a missed payment still shows

The RAP Guardrail

If all of your loans were disbursed on or after July 1, 2026, Federal Student Aid says the only income-driven plan is the Repayment Assistance Plan (RAP). The payment is 1 to 10 percent of adjusted gross income, divided by 12, reduced by $50 per dependent, and it may not be less than $10 a month. The term on that table is 30 years.

  • Compulsory $10 minimum
  • $10 a month is the floor

Parent PLUS Warning

Federal Student Aid says borrowers who currently have only parent PLUS loans are not eligible for an income-driven plan. You may be eligible if those loans were consolidated into a Direct Consolidation Loan before July 1, 2026. Parent PLUS loans are never eligible for RAP, including after consolidation or a double consolidation. Parent loans are not eligible for IBR or ICR if consolidated after July 1, 2026. PAYE never covers parent loans. ICR and PAYE end no later than July 1, 2028.

  • Consolidation date matters for IBR and ICR
  • RAP never covers parent PLUS loans

Delinquency vs. Default

Understanding the 270-day window.

Status Timeframe Consequence Strategic Cure
Delinquent Day 1 to 270 Credit Score Drop Ask your servicer
Default After Day 270 Wage Garnishment Loan Rehabilitation

Federal Student Aid says defaulted loans are not eligible for any income-driven plan. The same FAQ says loan rehabilitation can make a loan eligible for an IDR plan once it is no longer in default, if you make the payments in the rehabilitation agreement. Another path it names is consolidating a defaulted loan into a Direct Consolidation Loan and agreeing to repay that new loan under RAP.

Questions

What is the RAP monthly floor?

Federal Student Aid says a RAP payment is 1 to 10 percent of adjusted gross income, divided by 12, reduced by $50 for each dependent, and it may not be less than $10 a month. The table lists a 30-year term.

Are parent PLUS loans eligible for RAP?

No. Direct PLUS loans for parents cannot be repaid under RAP. That includes a consolidation or a double consolidation that paid off a parent PLUS loan.

Can parent PLUS loans use another IDR plan?

Federal Student Aid says borrowers who have only parent PLUS loans are not eligible for an IDR plan unless those loans were consolidated into a Direct Consolidation Loan before July 1, 2026. Parent loans are not eligible for IBR or ICR if consolidated after July 1, 2026. PAYE never covers parent loans. ICR and PAYE end no later than July 1, 2028.
