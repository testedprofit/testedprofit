Botero's Man and Woman Sculpture in Denver

Botero Denver searches lead to the Man and Woman sculpture: two bronze figures by Fernando Botero in the galleria of the Denver Performing Arts Complex, at 14th and Curtis, outside the Temple Hoyne Buell Theatre. Denver Arts and Venues lists them as a 1998 public sculpture. The pair is free to view from the public galleria. A street address used for the block is 998 14th St, Denver, CO 80202.

The sculpture

The Man and Woman sculpture is a pair of bronzes in alcoves along the promenade in front of the Buell Theatre. Denver Arts and Venues public-art walks start at Man and Woman, in the galleria near 14th and Curtis. Visit Denver places them with theatergoers outside the Temple Hoyne Buell Theatre. The figures use Botero's exaggerated, rounded proportions. You can see them without a ticket for a show.

Published heights come in two numbers. The Denver Post describes each bronze at 13 feet. The Denver Gazette and Go Play Denver describe them as about 13 feet tall. A 2023 303 Magazine article said about 8 feet. Arts and Venues names the material, bronze, and the year 1998.

The year is published more than one way, and each account is specific. Denver Arts and Venues dates the bronzes to 1998. The Denver Post calls them 1998 bronzes that entered Denver's public art collection in 2000. The Denver Gazette of 15 September 2023 says the sculptures were installed in 1988 and, in the same story, that they have greeted visitors since 1983. 303 Magazine also says 1983. 303 Magazine is also the page that uses 998 14th St. That address geocodes to the 14th and Curtis block.

The artist, and a different Botero title

Fernando Botero made the pair. The rounded volumes are the look people expect from a Botero Denver stop: full forms, smooth bronze, and a scale that reads from across the galleria. Walk the alcoves slowly. Photographers usually step back into the promenade so both figures fit in one frame. The Buell Theatre doors are the backdrop. When a performance lets out, the bronzes become the meeting point Arts and Venues already built into the downtown public-art walk.

People sometimes search Botero Denver together with the title La Maternidad de Botero. The bronzes in this galleria are Man and Woman. La Maternidad de Botero is a different title, and this pair is the galleria work. Another Fernando Botero sculpture on this site is Fernando Botero: Madre e Hijo, a separate page from the Man and Woman sculpture.

From the galleria it is a short trip to Civic Center for the Bronco Buster Statue in Denver's Civic Center Park, to Champa Street for the Cowboy in Pajamas Sculpture in Denver, and to 30th and Downing for the Justina Ford Lady Doctor Statue in Denver. The Lakewood eagle is the American Eagle Statue in Lakewood. Use Denver Landmark Guides for the metro set, or go Back to Colorado for the state list.

Questions

Where is the Botero Denver Man and Woman sculpture?

In the Denver Performing Arts Complex galleria at 14th and Curtis streets, outside the Temple Hoyne Buell Theatre. A street address used for the block is 998 14th St, Denver, CO 80202.

Who made the Man and Woman sculpture, and when?

Fernando Botero. Denver Arts and Venues lists the bronzes in 1998. The Denver Post says those 1998 bronzes entered the public collection in 2000. The Denver Gazette of 15 September 2023 says they were installed in 1988 and have stood there since 1983. 303 Magazine also says 1983.

What is the Man and Woman sculpture made of, and how tall is it?

Bronze. The Denver Post describes each figure at 13 feet. The Denver Gazette and Go Play Denver describe about 13 feet. A 2023 303 Magazine article said about 8 feet.

Do I need a ticket to see Man and Woman?

The sculptures stand in the public galleria and are free to view. A theater ticket is for a performance, separate from walking up to the bronzes.

Is Man and Woman the same work as La Maternidad de Botero?

No. The Denver galleria pair is titled Man and Woman. La Maternidad de Botero is a different title. Madre e Hijo is a separate Botero sculpture on this site.
