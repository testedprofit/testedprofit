Our test: Content Delivery used more power and more bandwidth. In our test region, time marked Active did not consistently cover that use, because the device spent long stretches in the queue. We use standard bandwidth sharing. Details of that mode are on the Honeygain review.

Honeygain Content Delivery is a mode on the Honeygain homepage, in the section Ways the Honeygain earning app helps you earn. The card is labeled +144 and credits/24 hrs. The text says: Choose Content Delivery to make money by the hour. It is bandwidth-intensive and places are limited.

How the homepage places it next to other modes

The same section calls bandwidth sharing the main way to make passive income, and it says there is no daily limit on how much you can share. A daily-task card says share 5 MB of traffic to start rewards, and it marks the credits figure as a shown maximum. Referrals on that section pay up to $5 each time a friend reaches the $20 payout threshold. Achievements are a separate card for completed tasks.

What the features page says about content delivery

The features page describes a different unit. It says you earn credits for every megabyte shared. In the step about supporting data collection, it says trusted partners use the network for web statistics, content delivery, and market research. Later it says the connection supports tasks like content delivery and web performance testing, and that Honeygain does not use your files, messages, or browsing history.

Read together, the features page ties the words content delivery to the kind of work the network does, and it pays per megabyte. The homepage gives Content Delivery its own card, paid by the hour, with the label +144 credits/24 hrs and a limit on places. The $20 PayPal threshold, the $2 starting gift, and the device list are on the Honeygain review.
