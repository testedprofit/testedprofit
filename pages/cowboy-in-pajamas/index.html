Cowboy in Pajamas, a Cowboy Sculpture in Denver

Cowboy in Pajamas, also called Cowboy Pajamas, is one of the cowboy sculptures in downtown Denver: a cowboy bronze sculpture 20 feet tall by Sean O'Meallie. His site describes an abstract cowboy with its guns drawn. Place listings and a 30 July 2021 visitor account put the cowboy outside the Residence Inn Denver City Center at 1725 Champa St. A Colorado Springs Gazette story of 8 June 2008 said it would be installed that month. It is free to view from the sidewalk.

The artwork and its name

Sean O'Meallie's site calls Cowboy Pajamas a 20-foot painted bronze of an abstract cowboy with its guns drawn, in downtown Denver. The title on this guide, Cowboy in Pajamas, is the same work. Among cowboy sculptures in the city, this one is the painted figure in pajamas, guns drawn, tall enough to tower over people walking to the hotel. The Colorado College Fine Arts Center describes it as a 20-foot playfully abstracted cowboy in downtown Denver.

The pajamas are the artist's title. The Gazette said O'Meallie enlarged an earlier Cowboy Pajamas sculpture and described the stylized figure as a humorous, sometimes intellectual twist. Look for the cowboy, not a portrait of one named rider. The joke is the nightclothes and the drawn guns, at a scale that reads from the sidewalk.

How it got to Champa Street

The 8 June 2008 Gazette profile is the installation record. It says the sculpture was 20 feet tall and over 3 tons, would be installed that month on private land downtown, and was cast in China because an American foundry quote would have stopped the project. O'Meallie said the first time he would see it assembled and painted was at the installation. An anonymous donor asked for a work that would be on public view. The story presents it as a private commission with a public view.

A 30 July 2021 visitor account says it has stood outside the Residence Inn since 2008. The street address, 1725 Champa St, is the Residence Inn Denver City Center. Place listings and that visitor account are the ones that print the street. The map pin is the geocode for that address. Treat it as a sidewalk stop. There is no ticket.

Questions

Where is the Cowboy in Pajamas sculpture in Denver?

Place listings and a 30 July 2021 visitor account put it outside the Residence Inn Denver City Center at 1725 Champa St, Denver, CO 80202. Sean O'Meallie's site places Cowboy Pajamas in downtown Denver.

What is the official name of this cowboy sculpture?

Cowboy in Pajamas. The artist also calls the piece Cowboy Pajamas. It is a 20-foot painted bronze of an abstract cowboy with its guns drawn.

Who made this cowboy bronze sculpture, and how big is it?

Sean O'Meallie. His site calls it a 20-foot painted bronze. The Colorado Springs Gazette reported on 8 June 2008 that it was 20 feet tall and over 3 tons, and that it was cast in China.

When was Cowboy in Pajamas installed?

The Gazette story of 8 June 2008 said it would be installed in downtown Denver that month. A 30 July 2021 account says it has stood outside the Residence Inn since 2008.

Is the cowboy free to see?

There is no admission fee to look at it from the sidewalk. The Gazette said an anonymous donor commissioned it for private land that would be on public view.
