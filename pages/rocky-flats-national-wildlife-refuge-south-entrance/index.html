Rocky Flats South Entrance

The south side of Rocky Flats National Wildlife Refuge is the trailhead the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service describes as a small parking lot on the southern border. Visiting the refuge is free.

Trails from this entrance

The hiking page says the South Woman Creek Trail is 0.4 miles. It calls the trail moderate because of rolling terrain and hills, and says it connects with the Woman Creek Trail.

The Woman Creek Trail is 3.0 miles. The hiking page calls it moderate, says the surface is dirt, and says there are views of South Woman Creek and Upper and Lower Church Lake. It says to watch for elk, deer, raptors, and songbirds. The refuge location page uses the name Woman Creek Trail for the 3.0-mile route. It does not call that route a loop.

The refuge has 15 miles of trails in total. The other named trails on the hiking page, including Walnut Creek and Lindsay Ranch, are described from the main refuge entrance, not from this south lot.

Hours and rules

The location page and the rules page say designated trails and parking areas are open sunrise to sunset, and closed on Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day. Visitors are to stay on designated trails. Other refuge land and staff roads are closed.

Allowed activities on the rules page include wildlife observation, wildlife photography, hiking, snowshoeing, cross-country skiing, bicycling and Class 1 e-bikes, and horseback riding. Restrooms are described at the main entrance. The hiking page does not describe a restroom at the south lot.

The rules page says dogs are not allowed at Rocky Flats. It says service animals are allowed and must be on a leash, and that emotional-support, therapy, and comfort animals do not qualify. The location page's short list says only service dogs are allowed. The rules also prohibit drones, alcohol, marijuana, camping, overnight parking, campfires and grills, and swimming or bathing in refuge water.

The refuge

The location page says the refuge was established in 2007, covers 5,237 acres, and protects more than 630 plant species and the xeric tallgrass prairie. It says the land provides habitat for 239 migratory and resident wildlife species, including prairie falcons, deer, elk, coyotes, songbirds, and the threatened Preble's meadow jumping mouse. It describes a Cold War manufacturing site for nuclear weapons components, a cleanup completed in 2005, and a public opening in 2018.

The published refuge address on that page is 16500 W 120th Avenue, Superior, CO 80027. That is the main-entrance address, not a street address for the south parking lot. The hiking page does not print a street address for the south lot.

Last updated 21 October 2026.

Questions

Which trails start at the south entrance?

The refuge hiking page says the South Woman Creek Trail is 0.4 miles and starts at the small parking lot on the southern border. It connects with the Woman Creek Trail, which is 3.0 miles, dirt, and has views of South Woman Creek and Upper and Lower Church Lake.

Are dogs allowed at Rocky Flats?

The refuge rules say dogs are not allowed. Service animals are allowed on a leash. The location page says only service dogs are allowed.
