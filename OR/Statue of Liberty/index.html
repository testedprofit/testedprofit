Statue of Liberty

The Statue of Liberty in Milwaukie is a roadside landmark in a parking lot off Southeast McLoughlin Boulevard. Willamette Week's July 22, 2025 story says diners on the back deck at Arrivederci Wine and Jazz can see a scaled replica, 51 feet tall, standing in that parking lot. Chris Sanaee, an immigrant from Iran who owns one-quarter of the properties surrounding the statue, installed it in 2003. He told the paper he felt that things were falling apart in the country after September 11, and that he wanted something on the property to bring people together and remind them of what they have.

The same interview says the statue caught fire during installation because of a welding error. Sanaee ordered and erected a new one. Willamette Week describes the figure that stands there now as a reminder of American ideals, and it quotes Sanaee saying his deep-down feeling is the same while a lot has changed in the last 20 years. The story places the replica in Milwaukie, visible from the restaurant deck on Southeast McLoughlin Boulevard, and it gives the height as 51 feet.

Sanaee owns a share of the property around the figure. The paper's account is how the 51-foot replica came to stand in that Milwaukie parking lot in 2003, caught fire while it was going up, and was replaced. The replica is the landmark visitors look for behind the McLoughlin Boulevard storefronts.
