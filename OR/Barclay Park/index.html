Barclay Park

Barclay Park is a roadside landmark in downtown Sisters, Oregon. Travel Oregon, using a Sisters Chamber listing, says the park is named for Harold and Dorothy Barclay.

Wikipedia places Sisters in Deschutes County, in the Bend metropolitan area, with a population of 3,064 at the 2020 census. The community takes its name from the Three Sisters mountains. Before European settlement, Paiute, Warm Springs, and Wasco people used trails through Warm Springs and across the McKenzie and Santiam passes. Camp Polk, about 3 miles from the present town, stood from September 1865 to May 1866. Samuel M. Hindman settled the area in 1870 and built a shop and a post office. In 1888 the post office moved to the John J. Smith Store, 3 miles south of Camp Polk.

The same article lists Oregon Route 126, Oregon Route 242, and U.S. Route 20 through the city, and Sisters Eagle Air Airport. Nearby recreation includes the Peterson Ridge Trail, Suttle Lake trails, Hoodoo ski resort, and snow parks. Barclay Park sits in that downtown, the park Travel Oregon names for Harold and Dorothy Barclay.
