The Dancing Mary

The Dancing Mary is a roadside landmark in Portland, Oregon. Bill Natkin's account, published October 12, 2024, places the statue in a Portland cemetery and says local folklore calls it the Dancing Mary or Moving Mary. He records the inscription Consolatrix Afflictorum on the figure.

Natkin writes that Our Lady of Consolation, or Mary, Comforter of the Afflicted, is a title the Catholic Church gives to the Virgin Mary, and that Consolatrix Afflictorum is the Latin form of that title. He says the Portland legend has been around a long time. In the versions he sets down, people say the statue has been seen dancing or moving late at night, and some tales say she haunts visitors.

He describes the cemetery as a hilltop where sound carries, close enough to his home that he knows the grounds draw people after dark. The photograph he published on October 12, 2024, is tagged Portland, Oregon, and shows the cemetery sculpture those stories call The Dancing Mary. The figure is the roadside landmark this page names, in that Portland cemetery, under the inscription he records.
